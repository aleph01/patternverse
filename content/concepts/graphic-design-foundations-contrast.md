A design can need several kinds of difference at once. Letters must separate from their ground, two peer categories must remain distinguishable, and a title may need more prominence than reference material. Calling all three problems insufficient contrast is a useful observation, but it does not select the repair.

**Contrast is a visible difference with a job.** First name the relationship that needs clarification, then choose the relevant design decision.

## Choose the repair for the distinction

- If content ranks are unclear, use [[graphic-design-establish-visual-hierarchy|Establish Visual Hierarchy]]. Size, weight, isolation or position can express the levels.
- If text or an object merges with its field, use [[graphic-design-separate-figure-from-ground|Separate Figure from Ground]]. Background detail and layering can matter as much as color.
- If the light–dark relationship is insufficient, use [[graphic-design-establish-value-contrast|Establish Value Contrast]]. A hue change alone may not repair it.
- If categories or roles are visually inconsistent, use [[graphic-design-signal-relationships-through-similarity|Signal Relationships Through Similarity]]. Make shared features carry dependable meanings.
- If hue is the only carrier of a necessary distinction, use [[graphic-design-reinforce-meaning-beyond-color|Reinforce Meaning Beyond Color]]. Add an independently interpretable cue.

## Contrast does not always mean emphasis

Two peer categories can differ without either becoming more important. A patterned line can distinguish a series while preserving its width and coordinates. A restrained typeface change can distinguish speakers without enlarging one voice. Conversely, every necessary item does not need maximum contrast: strengthening all elements can destroy the hierarchy that the design needs.

Judge the difference in its context and at its intended size. A distinction can be technically present yet insufficient for its actual task. Making it stronger can also create an unintended category or priority, so decide what the difference should mean before changing it.
