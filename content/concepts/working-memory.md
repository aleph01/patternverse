title: Working Memory
summary: The limited mental workspace used to hold and manipulate information.
status: published
verses:
  - learning
---

Working memory holds the small amount of information currently available for deliberate thought.
Because its capacity is limited, tasks become harder when too many unresolved details must be kept
active at once.

External representations, smaller steps, and clear cues can reduce this load.
