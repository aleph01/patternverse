Allocate uncertainty and responsibility between participants.

[[guaranteed-availability|Guaranteed Availability]] — Sell dependable operation backed by service responsibilities.

[[shared-savings|Shared Savings]] — Receive an agreed portion of verified customer savings.

[[guaranteed-buyback|Guaranteed Buyback]] — Reduce purchasing uncertainty through a future repurchase commitment.

[[consignment|Consignment]] — Retain ownership of inventory until a downstream sale.

[[take-or-pay|Take-or-Pay]] — Obtain a minimum payment commitment regardless of consumption.

[[reserved-capacity|Reserved Capacity]] — Hold specified capacity available for a customer.

[[commitment-for-discount|Commitment for Discount]] — Exchange better terms for dependable customer commitments.

[[interruptible-capacity|Interruptible Capacity]] — Sell lower-priced access that the provider may reclaim.
