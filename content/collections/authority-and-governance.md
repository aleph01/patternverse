Who may decide, within what limits, and how is responsibility maintained?

Explore patterns for distributing authority, retaining accountable ownership, and making decisions workable across organizational levels.

[[mission-command|Mission Command]] — Give capable teams a clear intent and bounded authority to adapt their actions as circumstances change, supported by trust and timely information.

[[teamwork-clarify-decision-rights|Explicit Decision Rights]] — Specify who prepares, contributes to, authorizes, and carries out important decisions, with clear limits and routes for exceptions.

[[delegated-domains|Delegated Domains]] — Give a person or group continuing authority over a defined area of work, with explicit boundaries, resources, dependencies, and review.

[[decide-close-to-the-work|Decide Close to the Work]] — Place decisions with the nearest capable people who can understand their effects, supported by clear authority, timely information, and a route for wider issues.

[[advice-process|Advice Process]] — Require a decision owner to seek and consider advice from affected people and relevant experts, then decide, explain, and remain accountable for the result.

[[consent-decision-making|Consent Decision-Making]] — Adopt workable proposals when no qualifying objection remains, using reasoned objections to improve the decision and explicit review to learn from it.

[[explicit-escalation-paths|Explicit Escalation Paths]] — Define when an issue needs wider help or authority, who receives it, how responsibility is accepted, and what happens if it remains unresolved.
