Let experience register, carry it into action, and close experiential work. These are related functions, not a prescribed sequence.

- [[savoring-a-resource|Savoring a Resource]] — Let a helpful experience register through attention to its particular bodily qualities.
- [[metaprocessing|Metaprocessing]] — Explore what it is like to have had a meaningful experience or change.
- [[finding-a-felt-right-action-step|Finding a Felt-Right Action Step]] — Find a small outward action that fits a newly sensed possibility.
- [[rehearsing-the-new-response|Rehearsing the New Response]] — Practice a new response within a concrete anticipated situation.
- [[de-roling|De-roling]] — Explicitly leave an enacted role and return to your own identity and the present setting.
