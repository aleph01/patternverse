Shared work depends on arrangements as well as intentions. Examine what action is already available, which rules protect the purpose, how a gathering invites participation, and how a relationship is experienced. The worksheets produce personal assessments and proposals. Naming another person in a plan does not create their commitment; relevant agreements remain part of the real work.
