Choose responses to instructions, repeated requests, disputed rules, or physical aggression.

- [[parenting-following-instructions|Not listening or following instructions]]: Separate attention, understanding, memory, and disagreement before choosing how to help a child follow directions.
- [[parenting-whining-and-repeated-demands|Whining and repeated demands]]: Respond to repeated requests while distinguishing unmet needs, negotiation, and a narrow role for selective attention.
- [[parenting-arguing-about-rules|Arguing about rules]]: Decide which rules need explanation, negotiation, revision, or a firm limit without requiring agreement.
- [[parenting-hitting-biting-and-pushing|Hitting, biting, or pushing]]: Protect people from physical aggression, investigate recurring triggers, and teach safer ways to act.
