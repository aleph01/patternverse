Define the rights customers receive and who owns the assets.

[[access-over-ownership|Access over Ownership]] — Organize an offering around use rather than possession.

[[rent-instead-of-buy|Rent Instead of Buy]] — Provide temporary use while retaining ownership.

[[leasing|Leasing]] — Provide asset access through an agreed contractual term.

[[fractional-ownership|Fractional Ownership]] — Divide ownership and usage rights among customers.

[[shared-access|Shared Access]] — Let multiple customers use a common resource pool.

[[product-as-a-service|Product as a Service]] — Provide a product’s function with continuing provider responsibility.

[[membership|Membership]] — Provide participation, privileges, resources, or relationships.
