Organize the activities that create and deliver value.

[[vertical-integration|Vertical Integration / Integrator]] — Bring connected stages of value creation within one business.

[[orchestrator|Orchestrator]] — Coordinate specialist partners around an overall offering.

[[layer-player|Layer Player]] — Specialize in one activity across multiple value chains.

[[self-service|Self-Service]] — Enable customers to perform selected activities themselves.

[[productized-service|Productized Service]] — Make a service clearly scoped and repeatable.

[[managed-service|Managed Service]] — Assume continuing responsibility for operating a customer function.

[[demand-driven-production|Demand-Driven Production / From Push to Pull]] — Organize activity around actual customer demand.

[[automatic-replenishment|Automatic Replenishment / Object Self-Service]] — Let connected products initiate replenishment or related orders.
