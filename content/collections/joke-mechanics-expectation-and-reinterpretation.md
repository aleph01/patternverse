A later beat changes the audience’s understanding of what came before.

5 structures, with 15 examples.

- [[joke-reversal|Reversal]] — Establish a direction or expectation, then turn it toward its opposite.
- [[joke-assumption-shift|Assumption Shift]] — Withhold a detail, then reveal that the audience supplied the wrong one.
- [[joke-comic-self-correction|Comic Self-Correction]] — Revise your own statement into something less flattering or more revealing.
- [[joke-anticlimax|Anticlimax]] — Build toward a substantial revelation, then deliver a disproportionately small payoff.
- [[joke-reassurance-with-an-alarming-qualifier|Reassurance with an Alarming Qualifier]] — Offer comfort, then add a restriction that introduces a worse concern.
