Discover the meanings and emotional consequences organizing a response. These are related functions, not a prescribed sequence.

- [[systematic-evocative-unfolding|Systematic Evocative Unfolding]] — Revisit a puzzling reaction in enough detail for its personal significance to emerge.
- [[finding-the-feeling-beneath-the-reaction|Finding the Feeling Beneath the Reaction]] — Distinguish an emotional response from reactions to having that response.
- [[symptom-deprivation|Symptom Deprivation]] — Explore what becomes emotionally difficult when a familiar symptom is absent in imagination.
- [[sentence-completion|Sentence Completion]] — Let an emotionally relevant unfinished sentence reveal a previously unarticulated meaning.
- [[overt-statement|Overt Statement]] — Speak from implicit emotional knowledge in a candid statement that fits lived experience.
