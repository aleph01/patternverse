Determine what customers pay for and how charges are calculated.

[[outright-sale|Outright Sale]] — Transfer ownership for an agreed price.

[[time-and-materials|Time and Materials]] — Charge for work performed and resources consumed.

[[subscription|Subscription]] — Charge periodically for continuing value.

[[pay-per-use|Pay per Use]] — Connect payment to measured consumption.

[[per-seat-pricing|Per-Seat Pricing]] — Scale charges with the number of users.

[[flat-rate-access|Flat-Rate Access]] — Provide a defined scope for a predictable fixed charge.

[[retainer|Retainer]] — Reserve expertise, availability, or service capacity.

[[outcome-based-pricing|Outcome-Based Pricing]] — Connect payment to a measurable result.

[[dynamic-pricing|Dynamic Pricing]] — Adjust prices as relevant conditions change.

[[credits|Credits]] — Represent consumption through purchasable units.

[[pay-what-you-want|Pay What You Want]] — Let customers determine their contribution.

[[barter|Barter]] — Exchange useful goods, services, or rights without direct monetary payment.
