Investigate the practical, emotional, and physical barriers behind recurring difficulties with everyday routines.

- [[parenting-bedtime-battles|Bedtime battles]]: Choose responses that fit sleep timing, repeated requests, fear, or difficulty settling at night.
- [[parenting-picky-eating|Picky eating]]: Support adequate eating and low-pressure learning while checking for pain, swallowing difficulties, or nutritional concerns.
- [[parenting-screen-time-conflicts|Screen-time conflicts]]: Balance digital enjoyment and connection with sleep, family routines, stopping skills, and growing autonomy.
- [[parenting-homework-resistance|Homework resistance]]: Distinguish task difficulty, starting problems, fear, and unreasonable workload before choosing homework support.
- [[parenting-stopping-and-transitions|Difficulty stopping an enjoyable activity or moving between activities]]: Help a child leave absorbing activities and start the next task by addressing uncertainty, loss, and practical barriers.
- [[parenting-dressing-washing-and-teeth|Resistance to dressing, washing, or brushing teeth]]: Distinguish discomfort, sensory demands, skill gaps, and autonomy in daily personal-care routines.
