Make experience accessible through different positions, voices, and roles. These are related functions, not a prescribed sequence.

- [[empty-chair-dialogue|Empty-Chair Dialogue]] — Address an imagined significant other to explore unresolved feelings and needs.
- [[doubling|Doubling]] — Lend a tentative first-person voice to experience that its owner can correct or reject.
- [[role-reversal|Role Reversal]] — Temporarily inhabit another role to explore an encounter from a different position.
- [[seeing-yourself-from-outside|Seeing Yourself from Outside]] — Observe a representation of your own role in an enacted scene.
- [[trying-the-disowned-direction|Trying the Disowned Direction]] — Explore unfamiliar dream qualities that challenge a habitual interpretation.
- [[reclaiming-a-projected-quality|Reclaiming a Projected Quality]] — Tentatively explore whether a quality attributed to another also has a place in yourself.
