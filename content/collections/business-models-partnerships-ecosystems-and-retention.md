Create value with partners and sustain participation over time.

[[open-business-model|Open Business Model]] — Make external collaboration central to value creation.

[[joint-venture|Joint Venture]] — Establish a jointly owned vehicle for shared activity.

[[brands-consortium|Brands Consortium]] — Pool resources across brands for mutually useful initiatives.

[[revenue-sharing|Revenue Sharing]] — Connect participant rewards to jointly generated revenue.

[[ingredient-branding|Ingredient Branding]] — Make a component valuable and recognizable to downstream customers.

[[oem-design-in|OEM Design-In]] — Incorporate a component or technology into another manufacturer’s product.

[[extension-ecosystem|Extension Ecosystem]] — Enable third parties to create complementary offerings.

[[customer-loyalty-program|Customer Loyalty Program]] — Reward continued purchasing or participation.

[[lock-in|Lock-in]] — Create switching costs through accumulated dependencies or commitments.

[[cross-selling|Cross-Selling]] — Serve additional needs through an existing customer relationship.
