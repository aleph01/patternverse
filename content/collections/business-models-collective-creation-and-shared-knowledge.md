Involve others in ideas, production, development, and research.

[[crowdsourcing|Crowdsourcing]] — Draw contributions from a distributed group.

[[bounties|Bounties]] — Reward contributors for achieving specified outcomes.

[[user-designed-products|User-Designed Products]] — Commercialize designs contributed by users or creators.

[[prosumer|Prosumer]] — Enable customers to participate in production.

[[customer-funded-development|Customer-Funded Development]] — Fund development through customers while retaining agreed reuse rights.

[[syndicated-research|Syndicated Research]] — Conduct research once for multiple paying customers.
