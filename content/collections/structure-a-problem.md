Use this collection when explanations and solutions are competing before the question is clear.

[[ishikawa-diagram]] inventories possible causes of a specific effect across categories. [[issue-trees]] decomposes a question into answerable branches, checks omissions and overlap, and selects an investigation. Neither representation proves a cause.

[[first-principles-thinking]] separates supported requirements from conventions, then rebuilds and tests a solution. It complements [[reframing]], which helps reconsider what problem to work on.

Choose the structure that fits the task. You do not need to finish every worksheet before gathering a useful observation.
