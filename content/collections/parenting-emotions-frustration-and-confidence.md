Start here when blocked goals, losing, difficult tasks, or mistakes lead to distress or avoidance.

- [[parenting-frequent-tantrums|Frequent tantrums]]: Understand recurring outbursts and choose between immediate protection, comfort, prevention, and changes to recurring interactions.
- [[parenting-losing-and-disappointment|Difficulty Losing or Accepting Disappointment]]: Help a child handle losing and disappointment while separating competition, skill demands, and fears about being good enough.
- [[parenting-giving-up-when-things-are-hard|Giving Up Quickly When Something Is Hard]]: Distinguish missing skills, overwhelm, and fear of failure when a child abandons difficult tasks.
- [[parenting-fear-of-mistakes-and-perfectionism|Fear of mistakes or needing everything to be perfect]]: Respond to fear of mistakes without reinforcing endless checking, avoidance, or pressure to perform.
