Delegate and Coordinate examines the operating agreements that make agent work useful within a larger engineering effort.

[[agentic-bound-the-task|Bound the Task]] connects outcomes, permitted effects, available resources, decision triggers, and evidence for a handoff. Its examples distinguish preview work from production writes, a local package update from a service-contract change, and draft editing from live publication.

[[agentic-deliver-small-reviewable-changes|Deliver Small, Reviewable Changes]] splits work by coherent purpose and evidence, retaining dependencies, shared contracts, and a working integrated state.

[[agentic-coordinate-concurrent-work|Coordinate Concurrent Work]] combines shared contracts, explicit edit areas, workspace isolation, and integration responsibility, while recognizing when tightly coupled work should be sequential.

The collection contains authored drafts; further entries will appear as their articles are completed.
