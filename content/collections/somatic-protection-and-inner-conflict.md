Understand protective processes and facilitate communication between conflicting positions. These are related functions, not a prescribed sequence.

- [[unblending|Unblending]] — Relate to a feeling or part without being entirely absorbed in its perspective.
- [[getting-permission-from-the-protector|Getting Permission from the Protector]] — Understand protective concerns before negotiating access to vulnerable experience.
- [[taking-over-the-defense|Taking Over the Defense]] — Support an existing protective effort with permission so other experience can become accessible.
- [[two-chair-dialogue|Two-Chair Dialogue]] — Give conflicting inner positions separate voices and let them respond to each other.
  - Enacting the Interruption (original reference 34), included in the parent article.
- [[mediating-polarized-parts|Mediating Polarized Parts]] — Help opposing protective positions understand each other without choosing a winner.
