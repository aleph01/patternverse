Steer and Recover connects implementation decisions to observations that can change the next action.

[[agentic-steer-through-evidence|Steer Through Evidence]] selects representative probes, distinguishes competing diagnoses, and preserves the limits of prototype evidence before further implementation accumulates.

[[agentic-keep-work-recoverable|Keep Work Recoverable]] pairs restorable work state with retained failure evidence, distinguishes source recovery from external-effect repair, and anchors a fresh session in actual changes.
