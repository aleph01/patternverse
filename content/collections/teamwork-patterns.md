These patterns address recurring difficulties in working together. Begin with the difficulty you recognize: too much unfinished work, hidden dependencies, unclear decisions, concentrated knowledge, or conversations that leave different understandings. Each article explains a practical response, develops hypothetical examples, and identifies sources and limits.
