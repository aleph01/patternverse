Determine how offerings reach customers and where purchases happen.

[[direct-to-customer|Direct to Customer / No Middle Man]] — Own the direct selling relationship.

[[e-commerce|E-commerce]] — Conduct purchasing through digital channels.

[[omnichannel|Omnichannel]] — Connect customer journeys across channels.

[[shop-in-shop|Shop-in-Shop]] — Operate an offering within another business’s environment.

[[dropshipping|Dropshipping]] — Sell while a supplier fulfills directly to the customer.

[[affiliate-commerce|Affiliate Commerce / Affiliation]] — Earn from attributable referrals to another seller.

[[product-as-point-of-sale|Product as Point of Sale]] — Enable purchasing at the point of use.

[[broad-assortment-retail|Broad-Assortment Retail / Supermarket]] — Combine wide selection with convenient consolidated shopping.
