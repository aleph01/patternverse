Explore new emotional experience alongside established expectations and losses. These are related functions, not a prescribed sequence.

- [[receiving-the-missing-experience|Receiving the Missing Experience]] — Explore receiving a previously missing kind of support while respecting barriers to receiving.
- [[compassionate-self-soothing|Compassionate Self-Soothing]] — Bring an accessible caring response into contact with a vulnerable aspect of yourself.
- [[imagery-rescripting|Imagery Rescripting]] — Introduce protection, agency, or care into an imagined scene to explore changed emotional meanings.
- [[discriminating-then-from-now|Discriminating Then from Now]] — Notice concrete differences between a present encounter and an earlier threatening context.
- [[juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]] — Bring a specific emotional expectation together with personally convincing contradictory knowledge.
- [[mourning-the-self|Mourning the Self]] — Allow grief about what was endured or missed to accompany growing self-understanding.
