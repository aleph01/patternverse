Verify and Review examines the evidence supporting a change and the judgments needed to accept it.

[[agentic-establish-independent-checks|Establish Independent Checks]] derives expected results from requirements and references, retains the same regression case across a repair, and distinguishes changed behavior from weakened assertions.

[[agentic-verify-the-complete-behavior|Verify the Complete Behavior]] follows representative outcomes through actual interfaces, persistent state, fresh reads, and specified failure behavior while retaining a proportionate portfolio of checks.

[[agentic-review-intent-and-risk|Review Intent and Risk]] accounts for the whole change, traces consequential behavior against intent, and validates reviewer claims before assigning a disposition.
