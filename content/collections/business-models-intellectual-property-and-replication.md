Commercialize rights, knowledge, brands, and repeatable systems.

[[licensing|Licensing]] — Grant defined usage rights for fees or royalties.

[[franchising|Franchising]] — Replicate an operating system through independent operators.

[[white-label|White Label]] — Supply offerings that partners sell under their brands.

[[open-source|Open Source]] — Enable use, inspection, modification, and redistribution under open-source terms.

[[open-source-with-paid-services|Open Source with Paid Services]] — Earn through services surrounding openly available software.

[[open-core|Open Core]] — Combine an open-source core with paid proprietary capabilities.

[[dual-licensing|Dual Licensing]] — Offer alternative licensing terms for different usage needs.

[[patent-pool|Patent Pool / Collective Licensing]] — Coordinate access to rights held by multiple owners.

[[reverse-engineering|Reverse Engineering]] — Learn from an existing offering’s construction or behavior to develop compatible or alternative solutions.
