Part of [[graphic-design|Graphic Design]]. How can typography make language readable, expressive, and visually structured?

## Start with the reading problem

Different symptoms need different adjustments. Choose the closest match before changing several settings at once.

| What is happening? | Begin here |
| --- | --- |
| The letterforms are hard to distinguish, or required characters are missing | [[graphic-design-match-typeface-to-context]] |
| Text sizes drift, or headings and supporting text have unclear roles | [[graphic-design-establish-a-type-scale]] |
| Lines stretch too far or break sentences into tiny fragments | [[graphic-design-control-line-length]] |
| Lines crowd together or seem to fall into separate strips | [[graphic-design-adjust-line-spacing]] |
| Letters collide or words look pulled apart | [[graphic-design-adjust-letter-spacing]] |
| A title breaks at an awkward phrase, or a paragraph ends with a stranded word | [[graphic-design-control-line-breaks]] |

If individual text blocks read well but their order on the page is unclear, use [[graphic-design-establish-a-clear-reading-order|Establish a Clear Reading Order]]. For another kind of problem, return to [[graphic-design-patterns|the Graphic Design problem guide]].

## Explore the typography patterns

[[graphic-design-match-typeface-to-context|Match Typeface to Context]] — choose a typeface and its appropriate cut for the reader’s task, intended voice, required characters, and actual viewing and production conditions.

[[graphic-design-establish-a-type-scale|Establish a Type Scale]] — choose a limited set of type sizes, assign them to recurring text roles, and adapt the relationships to the medium while preserving readable supporting text.

[[graphic-design-control-line-length|Control Line Length]] — set the width available to prose so lines support the reading task, allowing natural reflow while preserving readable type and accommodating the surrounding layout.

[[graphic-design-adjust-line-spacing|Adjust Line Spacing]] — set the interval between lines to support the reading task, separating lines enough to follow while keeping connected text cohesive.

[[graphic-design-apply-optical-alignment|Apply Optical Alignment]] — make small, shape-aware positioning adjustments when numerical alignment leaves visible text edges or centers looking uneven.

[[graphic-design-adjust-letter-spacing|Adjust Letter Spacing]] — adjust spacing across a text run or between a particular pair to suit its letterforms, size and role while preserving coherent words and necessary word boundaries.

[[graphic-design-control-line-breaks|Control Line Breaks]] — choose where text continues onto a new line so phrasing, meaningful expressions and paragraph endings support the reading task without sacrificing adaptable layout.

[[graphic-design-use-type-as-image|Use Type as Image]] — make letterforms, their arrangement or their interior spaces carry a relevant visual idea while preserving an appropriate route to reading the message.
