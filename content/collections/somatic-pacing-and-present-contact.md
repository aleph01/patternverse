Keep experience approachable through manageable intensity and contact with the current setting. These are related functions, not a prescribed sequence.

- [[orienting|Orienting]] — Recover contact with present surroundings through exploratory sensory attention.
- [[clearing-a-space|Clearing a Space]] — Acknowledge several concerns without having to enter or solve them immediately.
- [[titration|Titration]] — Approach charged experience in manageable increments with room to settle.
- [[pendulation|Pendulation]] — Allow movement between activation and experiences of support or settling.
