Explore 128 business patterns across 16 families. Each article starts with what it is for and explains the mechanism, examples, economics, and limits. Patterns can be combined: a business can use several at once.

[[business-models-market-position-and-customer-value|Market Position & Customer Value]] — Choose whom to serve and what makes the offering valuable.

[[business-models-offer-design-and-packaging|Offer Design & Packaging]] — Arrange products, services, features, and complementary purchases.

[[business-models-payment-and-pricing|Payment & Pricing]] — Determine what customers pay for and how charges are calculated.

[[business-models-access-ownership-and-membership|Access, Ownership & Membership]] — Define the rights customers receive and who owns the assets.

[[business-models-funding-working-capital-and-resource-economics|Funding, Working Capital & Resource Economics]] — Finance activity, assemble demand, and commercialize existing resources.

[[business-models-third-party-funding-and-cross-subsidy|Third-Party Funding & Cross-Subsidy]] — Enable someone other than the direct beneficiary to support the offering.

[[business-models-risk-guarantees-and-capacity-commitments|Risk, Guarantees & Capacity Commitments]] — Allocate uncertainty and responsibility between participants.

[[business-models-platforms-markets-and-intermediation|Platforms, Markets & Intermediation]] — Enable participants to find one another, interact, and transact.

[[business-models-distribution-and-points-of-sale|Distribution & Points of Sale]] — Determine how offerings reach customers and where purchases happen.

[[business-models-production-and-service-delivery|Production & Service Delivery]] — Organize the activities that create and deliver value.

[[business-models-partnerships-ecosystems-and-retention|Partnerships, Ecosystems & Retention]] — Create value with partners and sustain participation over time.

[[business-models-intellectual-property-and-replication|Intellectual Property & Replication]] — Commercialize rights, knowledge, brands, and repeatable systems.

[[business-models-digital-data-and-infrastructure|Digital, Data & Infrastructure]] — Create value through digital delivery, information, sensing, and shared infrastructure.

[[business-models-collective-creation-and-shared-knowledge|Collective Creation & Shared Knowledge]] — Involve others in ideas, production, development, and research.

[[business-models-circular-value-and-product-lifecycles|Circular Value & Product Lifecycles]] — Earn through longer use, repeated circulation, recovery, and improvement.

[[business-models-trust-financial-and-institutional-services|Trust, Financial & Institutional Services]] — Supply assurance, financial functionality, transaction responsibility, or an institutional home.
