Sleep can make a difficulty feel beyond your control: you cannot simply decide what to dream. This collection brings together ways of working with sleep-related experiences while awake.

Start with [[rewrite-the-nightmare]] when a recurring nightmare keeps returning to the same threat or helpless ending. It introduces Imagery Rehearsal Therapy: revise the dream while awake, then rehearse the changed version during the day.

The practice is an educational self-help adaptation. If engaging with the nightmare causes overwhelming distress, dissociation, or worsening symptoms, consider working with a clinician. Repeated nightmares or ongoing sleep disruption can also warrant professional assessment.
