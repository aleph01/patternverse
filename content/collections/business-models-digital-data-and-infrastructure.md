Create value through digital delivery, information, sensing, and shared infrastructure.

[[digitization|Digitization]] — Convert an offering or process into digital form.

[[virtualization|Virtualization]] — Reproduce a traditionally physical activity in a virtual environment.

[[platform-as-a-service|Platform as a Service]] — Provide shared platform capabilities others can build upon.

[[data-as-a-service|Data as a Service]] — Supply maintained datasets, feeds, or analytical access.

[[sensor-as-a-service|Sensor as a Service]] — Provide useful information and services through sensing.

[[leverage-customer-data|Leverage Customer Data]] — Improve offerings or generate insights from appropriately obtained customer information.

[[contribute-to-benchmark|Contribute-to-Benchmark]] — Turn participant contributions into comparative insights.
