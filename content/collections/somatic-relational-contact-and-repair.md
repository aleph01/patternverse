Establish connection and address disruptions in the relationship. These are related functions, not a prescribed sequence.

- [[loving-presence|Loving Presence]] — Cultivate appreciative attention that supports an unhurried, responsive therapeutic relationship.
- [[undoing-aloneness|Undoing Aloneness]] — Notice whether difficult experience can be felt in the company of an emotionally available other.
- [[turning-toward-the-other|Turning Toward the Other]] — Bring an accessible vulnerable feeling into a direct exchange with a partner.
- [[repairing-the-rupture|Repairing the Rupture]] — Explore relational strain and restore collaboration through acknowledgment and a changed response.
