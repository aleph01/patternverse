Twelve complementary patterns for exploring links between thoughts, feelings, and behavior. Start with the pattern that fits the situation—not with a requirement to work through all of them.

Use a thought record for a distressing interpretation, behavioral activation for low mood and withdrawal, or problem solving for a practical difficulty. Use the Double Standard Technique when self-criticism is harsher than the judgment you would offer a friend. Use the Semantic Method to turn a harsh self-directed should into a more accurate expression of what matters. Cognitive-distortion labels are tentative descriptions, including discounting the positive; they are not diagnoses or proof that your concerns are wrong.

Read a pattern or try its offline guided practice. Patterns available in Mira can also be selected there. These are original self-help adaptations, not a treatment program. Exposure is limited to planning manageable, objectively safe everyday steps; trauma, OCD, and physical-sensation work need qualified guidance. You remain in charge of pace and can stop at any point.
