A visible event rarely tells the whole story. Begin with [[iceberg-model]] to connect an event with recurrence, arrangements, and assumptions. Keep the evidence for each level separate.

Use [[connection-circles]] to identify changing variables and possible causal relationships. A map is a hypothesis to inspect, not proof that the system works as drawn.

[[balancing-feedback-loops]] examines responses that counteract change, including delay and overshoot. [[reinforcing-feedback-loops]] examines amplification, including decline and the limits that constrain growth. Read them together when a single loop fails to explain what you observe.

The practices preserve structured observations, relationships, and next investigations. They do not simulate systems or automatically establish causal-loop polarity.
