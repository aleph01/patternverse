These 23 patterns explore recurring designs for objects and their collaborations. Each article starts with a recognizable problem, explains the core mechanism, and develops concrete examples with syntax-highlighted code and explanatory illustrations. Applicability, costs, and neighboring designs help you decide whether the extra structure is worthwhile.

Browse the patterns alphabetically. The collection is a reference, so begin with the problem you are facing and follow comparisons between related designs.
