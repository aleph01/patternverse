A gathering can contain useful knowledge without giving it a route into the work. These patterns change how contributions become available: private preparation, repeated small exchanges, participant-proposed conversations, and listening across a difference. Choose an arrangement that serves the purpose and preserves voluntary participation. The guides prepare and debrief real-world activities; they do not coordinate participants through the app.
