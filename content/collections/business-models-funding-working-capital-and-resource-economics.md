Finance activity, assemble demand, and commercialize existing resources.

[[customer-funded-working-capital|Customer-Funded Working Capital / Cash Machine]] — Receive customer payments before associated supplier payments fall due.

[[crowdfunding|Crowdfunding]] — Assemble funding from many contributors through a defined campaign.

[[preorder-funding|Preorder Funding]] — Secure orders or payments before production and fulfillment.

[[microfinance|Microfinance]] — Provide financial services suited to customers underserved by conventional providers.

[[demand-aggregation|Demand Aggregation / Group Buying]] — Pool purchases to improve supply economics or commercial terms.

[[monetize-existing-capabilities|Monetize Existing Capabilities / Make More of It]] — Sell useful resources or capabilities beyond their original internal purpose.
