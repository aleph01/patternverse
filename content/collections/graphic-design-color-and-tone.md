Part of [[graphic-design|Graphic Design]]. How can color organize, differentiate, and express?

[[graphic-design-build-a-purposeful-palette|Build a Purposeful Palette]] — choose a working set of colors whose unity, variation and distinctions serve the communication, while preserving necessary tonal separation.

[[graphic-design-establish-value-contrast|Establish Value Contrast]] — adjust light-dark relationships so necessary text, boundaries and priorities separate from their surroundings, without giving every element maximum contrast.

[[graphic-design-reinforce-meaning-beyond-color|Reinforce Meaning Beyond Color]] — give color-coded information another visible carrier, such as words, shapes or line patterns, so the intended meaning remains available without identifying the hue.
