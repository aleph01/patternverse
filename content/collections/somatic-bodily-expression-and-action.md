Explore experience through posture, movement, gesture, sound, and collaborative experiments. These are related functions, not a prescribed sequence.

- [[experiments-in-mindfulness|Experiments in Mindfulness]] — Study the immediate effects of a small collaborative change in present experience.
  - Amplifying a Gesture (original reference 31), included in the parent article.
  - Physicalizing a Self-State (original reference 47), included in the parent article.
- [[embodying-emotions|Embodying Emotions]] — Explore Raja Selvam's approach to expanding the bodily experience of emotion, with support and integration.
- [[switching-channels|Switching Channels]] — Explore experience through another mode such as movement, sensation, imagery, or sound.
- [[moving-from-within|Moving from Within]] — Allow inner impulses to find movement or stillness in the presence of an attentive witness.
- [[exploring-the-action-that-wanted-to-happen|Exploring the Action That Wanted to Happen]] — Explore an emerging protective action at a manageable pace within supported therapeutic work.
- [[embodying-a-boundary|Embodying a Boundary]] — Discover how distance, gesture, posture, and movement express a personally meaningful boundary.
- [[accessing-protective-anger|Accessing Protective Anger]] — Contact an assertive response that recognizes the legitimacy of needs and boundaries.
