How do separate units make their interdependent work fit together?

Explore patterns for connecting specialist contributions, reconciling dependencies, and sustaining commitments across organizational boundaries.

[[integrator-roles|Integrator Roles]] — Assign a role to reconcile dependencies and tradeoffs across units so their separate contributions form a coherent shared result.

[[double-linking|Double Linking]] — Connect two groups through two distinct people who participate in governance decisions in both.

[[liaison-networks|Liaison Networks]] — Connect units through named contacts who exchange context, route questions, and maintain working relationships.

[[boundary-objects|Boundary Objects]] — Coordinate through shared artifacts that remain recognizable across groups while supporting their different uses.

[[dependency-mapping|Dependency Mapping]] — Make cross-unit dependencies explicit so people can see prerequisites, shared constraints, and the consequences of change.

[[team-api|Team API]] — Make a team’s responsibilities, offerings, and interaction expectations explicit and easy for other teams to use.
