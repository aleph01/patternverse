How do autonomous parts pursue a coherent direction?

Explore patterns for connecting shared purposes, evidence, priorities, and resource decisions while keeping local responsibilities clear.

[[shared-outcomes|Shared Outcomes]] — Align separate units around a result they must achieve together, with agreed measures, explicit contributions, and joint priority decisions.

[[hoshin-kanri|Hoshin Kanri]] — Turn a few strategic priorities into negotiated commitments across levels and functions, then review results and adjust through a recurring learning cycle.

[[strategic-guardrails|Strategic Guardrails]] — Make strategic boundaries, decision principles, and review triggers explicit so teams can choose locally without pulling the organization off course.

[[shared-strategic-measures|Shared Strategic Measures]] — Choose a small set of measures that units interpret together to judge strategic progress, expose tradeoffs, and adjust their contributions.
