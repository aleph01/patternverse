Distinguish considerate speech from concealing wrongdoing, and respond to unfair play without reducing the child to a label.

- [[parenting-white-lies-and-feelings|White lies to spare someone’s feelings]]: Help a child navigate kindness and honesty without making them responsible for protecting adults from every disappointment.
- [[parenting-cheating-to-win|Cheating to win]]: Respond to unfair play with clear rules, proportionate repair, and attention to what winning means to the child.
