Part of [[graphic-design|Graphic Design]]. How should photographs, illustrations, shapes, and symbols contribute to a composition?

[[graphic-design-choose-images-for-meaning|Choose Images for Meaning]] — select imagery for the specific activity, feature, subject or association the viewer needs to understand, and check that its visible content supports the surrounding message without implying unsupported facts.

[[graphic-design-crop-for-impact|Crop for Impact]] — adjust the visible window within an image to emphasize useful content, protect important outlines and retain necessary context, while keeping the framing truthful to its purpose.

[[graphic-design-integrate-text-and-image|Integrate Text and Image]] — coordinate the meaning, placement and connections of words and pictures so viewers can understand what belongs together while each remains useful and readable.

[[graphic-design-simplify-through-abstraction|Simplify Through Abstraction]] — replace incidental appearance with selected shapes, relationships or steps that preserve the information needed for a particular recognition or understanding task.

[[graphic-design-use-visual-metaphors|Use Visual Metaphors]] — borrow a recognizable quality or relationship from one subject to communicate another, making the intended comparison clear and checking the implications it brings with it.

[[graphic-design-employ-recognizable-symbols|Employ Recognizable Symbols]] — use visual signs whose meanings the intended audience can already recognize, match them to the actual service or action, and add words where the meaning remains uncertain.
