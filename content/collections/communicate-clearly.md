Clear communication requires more than concise wording. A listener needs to recognize the event, inspect the reasoning, and have room to respond.

[[situation-behavior-impact]] separates an occasion, observable behavior, and experienced impact before drafting feedback and an optional inquiry about intent.

[[minto-pyramid]] organizes an answer around supporting reasons and evidence. It helps reveal unsupported claims as well as improve the order of a message.

[[conflict-resolution-diagram]] makes opposing proposals, underlying needs, and the assumptions connecting them explicit. Use it when communication keeps returning to the same disagreement. A real resource conflict or incompatible interest may still require negotiation or a boundary.

The practices prepare messages and proposals; they do not send them or speak for other participants.


[[work-with-enduring-tensions]] helps articulate two legitimate demands that may continue to coexist. It complements inquiry into mistaken assumptions without promising that every disagreement disappears.
