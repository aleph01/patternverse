Specify the Work examines how to communicate an intended software change clearly enough to guide implementation and review.

[[agentic-specify-outcomes-with-examples|Specify Outcomes with Examples]] combines behavior rules with concrete starting conditions, actions, and expected results. Its worked cases cover CSV validation, search ordering and pagination, and a proportionate acceptance statement for a copy correction.

[[agentic-make-boundaries-and-decisions-explicit|Make Boundaries and Decisions Explicit]] distinguishes permitted changes, retained properties, assumptions, and unresolved choices. Its examples follow an overwrite policy, a dependency upgrade, and different prototype and production boundaries.

[[agentic-agree-on-done|Agree on Done]] connects task, feature, integration, and release claims to the evidence each needs, including proportionate completion for documentation.

The collection contains complete draft articles rather than placeholders for the planned catalogue.
