Separate ordinary disagreements and access disputes from exclusion, repeated harm, and changes in connection.

- [[parenting-sibling-rivalry|Sibling rivalry]]: Distinguish ordinary sibling conflict from recurring harm and choose between protection, mediation, and changes to family arrangements.
- [[parenting-sharing-and-taking-turns|Difficulty sharing and taking turns]]: Teach workable access and waiting arrangements while respecting ownership and developing self-control.
- [[parenting-exclusion-and-making-friends|Feeling excluded or struggling to make friends]]: Distinguish loneliness, a difficult peer setting, and missing opportunities or skills before choosing friendship support.
- [[parenting-arguments-with-friends|Frequent arguments with friends]]: Help a child understand recurring friendship conflict, attempt proportionate repair, and recognize when distance or protection fits better.
- [[parenting-being-teased-or-bullied|Being teased or bullied]]: Identify harmful peer patterns and coordinate protection without making the targeted child responsible for stopping them.
- [[parenting-teasing-or-excluding-others|Teasing or excluding other children]]: Stop harmful peer behavior, understand its social context, and support accountability without forcing closeness.
- [[parenting-withdrawal-and-communication|Withdrawal or reduced communication]]: Distinguish privacy and recovery time from a concerning change in connection, mood, or communication.
