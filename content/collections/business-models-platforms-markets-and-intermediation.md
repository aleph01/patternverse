Enable participants to find one another, interact, and transact.

[[multi-sided-platform|Multi-Sided Platform]] — Coordinate interactions between interdependent participant groups.

[[marketplace|Marketplace]] — Enable transactions between independent buyers and sellers.

[[managed-marketplace|Managed Marketplace]] — Add selected quality or fulfillment responsibilities to an exchange.

[[peer-to-peer|Peer-to-Peer]] — Enable individuals to exchange resources or services.

[[brokerage|Brokerage / Matchmaking]] — Arrange suitable transactions through expertise and relationships.

[[lead-generation|Lead Generation]] — Supply providers with qualified commercial opportunities.

[[auction|Auction]] — Allocate offerings through competitive bidding.

[[virtual-economy|Virtual Economy]] — Support exchanges involving virtual goods, services, or currencies.
