Choose whom to serve and what makes the offering valuable.

[[aikido|Aikido / Opposite Positioning]] — Attract customers by challenging an industry’s dominant assumptions.

[[bottom-of-the-pyramid|Bottom of the Pyramid]] — Serve customers whose needs are poorly addressed at affordable prices.

[[ultimate-luxury|Ultimate Luxury]] — Create value through exceptional quality, exclusivity, and service.

[[no-frills|No Frills]] — Deliver the essential benefit with fewer costly extras.

[[long-tail|Long Tail]] — Serve many small niches through a broad, discoverable assortment.

[[experience-selling|Experience Selling]] — Make the surrounding experience part of what customers purchase.

[[reverse-innovation|Reverse Innovation]] — Transfer solutions developed for constrained markets into other markets.

[[blended-value|Blended Value]] — Combine commercial value with social or environmental outcomes.
