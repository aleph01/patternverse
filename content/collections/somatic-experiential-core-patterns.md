These twelve articles explore recurring movements across somatic and experiential approaches: making contact possible, listening closely, including conflicting responses, discovering meaning, and allowing change to continue into everyday life. Each article develops the unifying move through approach comparisons, original fictional encounters, and source notes.

This is a draft reading edition. The order below is a way into the material, not a fixed treatment sequence. The patterns can recur, overlap, or be unnecessary in a particular encounter. No interactive practices are included.
