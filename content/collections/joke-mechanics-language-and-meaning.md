The laugh depends on how words are interpreted, combined or misused.

7 structures, with 21 examples.

- [[joke-double-entendre|Double Entendre]] — Make the same expression support two meanings.
- [[joke-sound-alike-substitution|Sound-Alike Substitution]] — Replace a word or phrase with a similar-sounding expression that changes the meaning.
- [[joke-literal-interpretation|Literal Interpretation]] — Treat an expression’s figurative or conventional meaning as literal.
- [[joke-twisted-cliche|Twisted Cliché]] — Establish a familiar expression, then change its expected continuation.
- [[joke-malapropism|Malapropism]] — Use the wrong similar-sounding word as an apparent mistake.
- [[joke-same-word-changed-meaning|Same Word, Changed Meaning]] — Repeat a word while changing its meaning: antanaclasis.
- [[joke-comic-definition|Comic Definition]] — Redefine something through a surprising description that captures a recognizable quality.
