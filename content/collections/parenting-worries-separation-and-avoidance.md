Distinguish unfamiliarity, repeated worry, separation distress, and difficulties connected specifically to school.

- [[parenting-avoiding-unfamiliar-activities|Avoiding unfamiliar activities]]: Choose between preparation, changes to the setting, gradual participation, and respecting a child's preferences.
- [[parenting-repeated-reassurance|Repeatedly asking for reassurance]]: Distinguish requests for information and connection from repeated attempts to eliminate uncertainty.
- [[parenting-separation-and-drop-off|Distress at separation or drop-off]]: Understand separation distress and plan a handover that fits the child's development and experience of care.
- [[parenting-school-reluctance-and-refusal|Reluctance or refusal to attend school]]: Investigate school avoidance and coordinate attendance support with changes to unsafe or unmanageable conditions.
