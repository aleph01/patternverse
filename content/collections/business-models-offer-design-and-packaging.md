Arrange products, services, features, and complementary purchases.

[[curation|Curation]] — Reduce customers’ selection effort through informed choice.

[[good-better-best|Good–Better–Best]] — Offer differentiated packages for different needs and budgets.

[[bundling|Bundling]] — Combine complementary offerings into one purchase.

[[unbundling|Unbundling]] — Let customers purchase selected components separately.

[[core-plus-add-ons|Core plus Add-ons]] — Provide a common foundation with optional paid extensions.

[[one-stop-shop|One-Stop Shop]] — Consolidate purchasing across a broad range of needs.

[[solution-provider|Solution Provider]] — Take responsibility for a broader customer problem.

[[mass-customization|Mass Customization]] — Deliver meaningful variation through standardized modules.

[[freemium|Freemium]] — Offer lasting free functionality alongside paid capabilities.

[[razor-and-blade|Razor and Blade / Bait and Hook]] — Support adoption through inexpensive entry and recurring complements.

[[reverse-razor-and-blade|Reverse Razor and Blade]] — Earn more upfront while keeping continuing supplies inexpensive.
