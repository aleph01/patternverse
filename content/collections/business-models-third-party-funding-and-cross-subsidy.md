Enable someone other than the direct beneficiary to support the offering.

[[third-party-funded-access|Third-Party-Funded Access / Hidden Revenue]] — Separate the user from the principal payer.

[[advertising-supported|Advertising-Supported]] — Fund an offering through advertising inventory or audience access.

[[sponsorship|Sponsorship]] — Fund activities through commercially valuable association.

[[cross-subsidized-access|Cross-Subsidized Access / Robin Hood]] — Use margins elsewhere to support affordable or free access.

[[voluntary-support|Voluntary Support / Donations and Patronage]] — Receive contributions from people who want the work to continue.
