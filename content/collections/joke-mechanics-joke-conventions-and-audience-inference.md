The laugh depends on expectations about jokes, conversations or omitted information.

5 structures, with 15 examples.

- [[joke-meta-joke|Meta-Joke]] — Make the joke’s construction, writing or performance part of its subject.
- [[joke-anti-joke|Anti-Joke]] — Invite the expectation of a conventional joke, then deliberately frustrate it.
- [[joke-implied-punchline|Implied Punchline]] — Give the audience enough information to complete the comic thought without stating it.
- [[joke-non-sequitur|Non Sequitur]] — Abruptly introduce a response or topic that does not follow from the established direction.
- [[joke-mentioning-by-refusing-to-mention|Mentioning by Refusing to Mention]] — Disclose something while pretending to omit it: paralipsis.
