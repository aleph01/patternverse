Contact experience, distinguish its components, and find expressions that fit. These are related functions, not a prescribed sequence.

- [[contacting-the-felt-sense|Contacting the Felt Sense]] — Let an initially unclear bodily sense of a whole situation form before explaining it.
- [[tracking-the-unfolding-experience|Tracking the Unfolding Experience]] — Follow the changing particulars of sensation, emotion, posture, meaning, and impulse.
  - Differentiating Sensation, Emotion, and Meaning (original reference 46), included in the parent article.
  - Exploring Double Signals (original reference 49), included in the parent article.
- [[finding-and-resonating-a-handle|Finding and Resonating a Handle]] — Find words or images that fit a felt sense and remain open to correction.
  - Drawing the Felt Sense (original reference 44), included in the parent article.
- [[experiential-reflection|Experiential Reflection]] — Offer language that someone can check against their own experience.
