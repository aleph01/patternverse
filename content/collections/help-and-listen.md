Useful help begins with an account that can be heard and questioned. These patterns support careful inquiry into actual successful episodes and consultation in which the recipient has room to listen and choose. Their outputs are observations, hypotheses, and selected next steps, rather than a diagnosis or a requirement to accept advice. Conversations happen with willing people outside the app.
