Games People Play brings together 36 illustrated articles on the games in Eric Berne’s main catalog. Each guide explains a recognizable interaction, the historical interpretation, possible openings for change, and circumstances in which the interpretation can mislead.

The articles distinguish Berne’s account from later transactional-analysis developments and original Patternverse examples and response suggestions. A game name is a way to examine an interaction, not a diagnosis of a person. Historical names are retained so readers can identify the source material; the articles examine dated or stigmatizing assumptions where they matter.

For a starting point, compare [[ta-why-dont-you-yes-but|Why Don’t You—Yes But]] with [[ta-im-only-trying-to-help-you|I’m Only Trying to Help You]]. They examine different ways an advice conversation can become stuck. [[ta-courtroom|Courtroom]] looks at inviting a third person to deliver a verdict, while [[ta-happy-to-help|Happy to Help]] examines useful help accompanied by another purpose.

## The catalog

The groups below follow the book’s historical categories. Their names describe the catalog’s organization rather than fixed classes of people or relationships.

### Life games

- [[ta-alcoholic|Alcoholic]] — Recognize recurring roles around harmful drinking while preserving care, choice, and responsibility.
- [[ta-debtor|Debtor]] — Keep a financial obligation from becoming a recurring contest over who is the wronged person.
- [[ta-kick-me|Kick Me]] — When an interaction seems to confirm an expectation of rejection
- [[ta-now-ive-got-you-you-son-of-a-bitch|Now I've Got You, You Son of a Bitch]] — When a real mistake becomes permission for a wider attack
- [[ta-see-what-you-made-me-do|See What You Made Me Do]] — When influence becomes an excuse to hand over responsibility

### Marital games

- [[ta-corner|Corner]] — When someone’s words become a reason to close off repair
- [[ta-courtroom|Courtroom]] — When getting help becomes a contest for a verdict
- [[ta-frigid-woman|Frigid Woman]] — A critical reading of a historical pattern around affection, expectation, and conflict
- [[ta-harried|Harried]] — When doing everything becomes the standard—and exhaustion becomes the familiar ending
- [[ta-if-it-werent-for-you|If It Weren't For You]] — When a restriction may also shelter a fear
- [[ta-look-how-hard-ive-tried|Look How Hard I've Tried]] — When participation becomes a defence against responsibility
- [[ta-sweetheart|Sweetheart]] — When an affectionate word makes a put-down harder to challenge

### Party games

- [[ta-aint-it-awful|Ain't It Awful]] — When shared dismay becomes the terms of connection
- [[ta-blemish|Blemish]] — When finding one fault becomes a reason to dismiss the person
- [[ta-schlemiel|Schlemiel]] — When an apology is expected to erase the consequences
- [[ta-why-dont-you-yes-but|Why Don’t You—Yes But]] — When asking for help becomes a contest over whether help is possible

### Sexual games

- [[ta-lets-you-and-him-fight|Let’s You and Him Fight]] — When two people are drawn into a contest that will not decide the outcome
- [[ta-perversion|Perversion]] — When an assumed prelude and the actual wish are different
- [[ta-rapo|Rapo]] — Pursuit, humiliation, and the danger of assuming motives
- [[ta-the-stocking-game|The Stocking Game]] — When attention becomes an argument about what other people are thinking
- [[ta-uproar|Uproar]] — When a quarrel makes distance easier than asking for it

### Underworld games

- [[ta-cops-and-robbers|Cops and Robbers]] — When the chase—and being caught—become part of the pattern
- [[ta-how-do-you-get-out-of-here|How Do You Get Out of Here]] — When departure is wanted—and becoming possible is frightening
- [[ta-lets-pull-a-fast-one-on-joey|Let’s Pull a Fast One on Joey]] — When the apparent accomplice discovers they are the target

### Consulting-room games

- [[ta-greenhouse|Greenhouse]] — When sharing feelings becomes an exhibition—and curiosity loses its place.
- [[ta-im-only-trying-to-help-you|I'm Only Trying to Help You]] — When unsuccessful help becomes proof that people are ungrateful.
- [[ta-indigence|Indigence]] — When a helping arrangement preserves the problem it is meant to address.
- [[ta-peasant|Peasant]] — When admiration becomes more important than finding out what helps.
- [[ta-psychiatry|Psychiatry]] — When treatment becomes a way to protect a role.
- [[ta-stupid|Stupid]] — When a difficulty becomes a verdict about a person.
- [[ta-wooden-leg|Wooden Leg]] — When an explanation becomes a blanket exemption—and the response becomes a contest.

### Good games

- [[ta-busmans-holiday|Busman's Holiday]] — Finding a different experience inside familiar work.
- [[ta-cavalier|Cavalier]] — The shared pleasure of a well-made compliment.
- [[ta-happy-to-help|Happy to Help]] — Useful generosity can serve another purpose. Both deserve attention.
- [[ta-homely-sage|Homely Sage]] — Being someone people can turn to—without needing every answer.
- [[ta-theyll-be-glad-they-knew-me|They’ll Be Glad They Knew Me]] — Making something good of the kindness people showed you.

## Context and sources

Transactional analysis supplies the broader model behind these descriptions. Its concepts include Parent, Adult, and Child ego states; transactions; recognition, described as strokes; and life scripts. Each article includes its own sources, fictional examples, limits, and an original illustration with a phone layout.

The catalog follows Eric Berne’s *Games People Play* (1964). The [Berne family’s game descriptions](https://ericberne.com/games-people-play/) and the [International Transactional Analysis Association’s overview](https://itaaworld.com/about-ta/) provide source context; the individual articles distinguish historical explanations from practical adaptations.
