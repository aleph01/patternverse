Part of [[graphic-design|Graphic Design]]. How does a design communicate its intended message?

[[graphic-design-define-the-core-message|Define the Core Message]] — decide what this audience should take away from this piece before choosing how to emphasize it.

[[graphic-design-design-for-the-audience|Design for the Audience]] — adapt language, information priority, and presentation to people's knowledge, tasks, and viewing conditions.

[[graphic-design-match-form-to-purpose|Match Form to Purpose]] — choose a structure and format that let people use the information for the task the design must support.

[[graphic-design-remove-the-nonessential|Remove the Nonessential]] — remove elements that do not earn their place in this encounter while preserving meaning, identity, and necessary conditions.

[[graphic-design-make-meaning-explicit|Make Meaning Explicit]] — state the relationship, scope, or consequence that a viewer would otherwise have to infer from an ambiguous design.

[[graphic-design-chunk-information|Chunk Information]] — organize material into useful units so readers can locate a part, understand what belongs together, and handle detail without reconstructing the structure.

[[graphic-design-sequence-the-message|Sequence the Message]] — arrange content so readers encounter context, dependencies, and supporting detail in an order suited to what they need to understand or do.
