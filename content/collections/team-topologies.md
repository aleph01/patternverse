How should teams divide responsibility and connect their work?

Explore patterns for defining team purposes, keeping responsibilities manageable, and choosing interactions that help useful work flow.

[[stream-aligned-team|Stream-Aligned Team]] — Give a stable team continuing responsibility for a manageable stream of value, from understanding needs through delivery and operation.

[[platform-team|Platform Team]] — Own shared capabilities as an internal product that helps other teams deliver with less complexity and coordination.

[[enabling-team|Enabling Team]] — Help teams acquire missing capabilities through focused facilitation, then step back as they become more self-sufficient.

[[complicated-subsystem-team|Complicated-Subsystem Team]] — Concentrate sustained specialist expertise in a bounded subsystem that other teams can use through a clear, supported interface.

[[time-limited-collaboration|Time-Limited Collaboration]] — Bring teams into close joint work for a defined discovery goal, then review and deliberately change how they interact.

[[x-as-a-service|X-as-a-Service]] — Provide a supported capability through a usable interface so other teams can make routine progress without repeated coordination.

[[facilitation|Facilitation]] — Help another team overcome a capability gap while it retains ownership, then reduce assistance as it can proceed for itself.
