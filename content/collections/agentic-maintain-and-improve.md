Maintain Understanding and Improve the Workflow keeps consequential engineering behavior and decisions available for review and future maintenance.

[[agentic-make-changes-understandable|Make Changes Understandable]] connects explanations to code responsibilities, representative inputs, failure behavior, and independently checked evidence without prescribing ceremonial explanation or unrelated rewrites.

[[agentic-improve-the-flow-of-accepted-work|Improve the Flow of Accepted Work]] follows useful outcomes through queues, dependencies, rework, integration, and acceptance, then tests a bounded change to the engineering process.
