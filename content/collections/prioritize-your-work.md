A deadline and a valuable contribution are different reasons to act. [[eisenhower-matrix]] separates urgency from importance and helps protect work that can otherwise be displaced indefinitely.

When comparing initiatives, [[impact-effort-matrix]] makes estimated contribution and effort visible on a map, then asks you to inspect uncertainty and dependencies before arranging priorities.

These are judgment aids rather than universal ranking rules. Obligations, access needs, and prerequisites should not disappear because an activity looks unattractive on a chart. For a specific consequential choice, continue with [[decision-making-patterns]].
