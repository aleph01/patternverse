Function and Architecture examines which elements perform which jobs and where the boundaries between those elements belong.

[[engineering-segmentation|Segmentation]] develops the choice of independently manageable units through a removable filter cartridge, a conveyor service section, and a vessel split that adds joints without a useful task.

[[engineering-standardized-module-interfaces|Standardized Module Interfaces]] specializes that choice by preserving the mating geometry, operating conditions, and signal meaning that suitable replacement units must share.

[[engineering-replaceable-wear-units|Replaceable Wear Units]] places predictable deterioration in a renewable liner, sleeve, or contact element while preserving suitable supporting equipment.

[[engineering-functional-separation|Functional Separation]] assigns incompatible jobs to distinct elements and makes their connecting interfaces explicit, through pump, measurement-enclosure, and sensor-cable examples.

[[engineering-function-integration|Function Integration]] combines compatible jobs in one component and weighs the removed assembly interfaces against manufacturing and renewal consequences. Function Transfer is its direct specialization.

[[engineering-function-transfer|Function Transfer]] specializes integration: an existing element takes over a dedicated part’s retained job, and that dedicated part is removed. The chassis and lubrication-drive cases test full service, combined loading, and availability.

[[engineering-eliminate-a-supporting-task|Eliminate a Supporting Task]] removes the design feature creating a supporting requirement, illustrated by a pump without a moving penetration and a guide without rubbing pivots. It is an independent terminal pattern in this family.
