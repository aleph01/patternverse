The laugh comes from a relationship between different things.

6 structures, with 20 examples.

- [[joke-comic-analogy|Comic Analogy]] — Use an unexpected comparison to make a recognizable relationship easier to see.
- [[joke-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]] — Put contrasting elements next to one another so the mismatch becomes the joke.
- [[joke-mirrored-phrase|Mirrored Phrase]] — Reverse repeated words or elements so their new order changes the relationship.
- [[joke-backhanded-compliment|Backhanded Compliment]] — Deliver praise that contains or becomes an insult.
- [[joke-parody-of-a-familiar-format|Parody of a Familiar Format]] — Reproduce a recognizable format with content that exposes or clashes with its conventions.
- [[joke-context-transplant|Context Transplant]] — Make the rules, habits or characters of one setting operate in another.
