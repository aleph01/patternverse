These three pilot articles examine six passages from official performance clips by Mitch Hedberg,
Gary Delaney, Taylor Tomlinson, and Eddie Izzard. Each article combines close reading of professional
material with an original worked revision, useful situations, and limitations.

Start with [[standup-misdirection|Misdirection]] when a payoff feels predictable; use
[[standup-add-tags|Add Tags]] when a joke has more possibilities within its established context;
read [[standup-let-the-audience-finish|Let the Audience Finish]] when explaining the implication
seems to weaken it. The articles are a repertoire, rather than a required sequence.

Professional examples are discussed through short excerpts and paraphrase. Clip timings refer to
the linked uploads. Research checked their timestamped caption tracks and source identities;
interpretations of mechanism are editorial readings, not accounts of the comedians' intentions.
