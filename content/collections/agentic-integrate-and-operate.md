Integrate and Operate connects agent-produced changes to shared history, delivery, and the systems that run them.

[[agentic-keep-a-traceable-change-history|Keep a Traceable Change History]] preserves intent, important decisions, coherent source changes, and version-specific evidence through review, integration, and release records.

[[agentic-integrate-frequently|Integrate Frequently]] keeps divergence short, combines independent work in dependency order, and verifies behavior on the resulting shared version.

[[agentic-release-with-observation-and-recovery|Release with Observation and Recovery]] controls user exposure, observes meaningful outcomes, preserves version compatibility, and distinguishes containment from persistent-effect repair.
