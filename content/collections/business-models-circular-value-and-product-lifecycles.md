Earn through longer use, repeated circulation, recovery, and improvement.

[[waste-to-value|Waste-to-Value / Trash-to-Cash]] — Recover commercial value from discarded resources.

[[by-product-valorization|By-product Valorization]] — Turn production residuals into useful commercial inputs.

[[resale|Resale / Recommerce]] — Return previously owned products to the market.

[[repair-and-maintenance|Repair and Maintenance]] — Earn by preserving or restoring useful function.

[[refurbishment|Refurbishment]] — Restore used products to a defined saleable condition.

[[remanufacturing|Remanufacturing]] — Rebuild products or components to specified performance standards.

[[closed-loop-asset-pooling|Closed-Loop Asset Pooling]] — Operate reusable assets through collection and redistribution networks.

[[deposit-return-system|Deposit-Return System]] — Encourage recovery through refundable deposits.

[[modular-upgrade-revenue|Modular Upgrade Revenue]] — Sell improvements while preserving useful existing components.

[[industrial-symbiosis|Industrial Symbiosis]] — Connect complementary resource flows between operations.

[[resale-as-a-service|Resale as a Service]] — Operate another business’s resale activities.
