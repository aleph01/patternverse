Writing improves through choices that serve a particular reader, piece, and intended effect. Explore nine families of techniques, each with original worked examples, source notes, and limits. You can begin with the problem in front of you rather than follow a fixed course.

The articles describe possibilities to test, rather than universal rules. The effect of a choice depends on its material, context, and purpose.
