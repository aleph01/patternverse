Assumptions and limits

Signals and weights are invented on a common teaching scale, not learned or calibrated probabilities. Both branches refer to damage for the same claim. With both present, the weights sum to one. A missing photo uses report weight one; it is not replaced with a zero signal. A present photo from another claim blocks scoring before any combination. ID checks cannot verify the photograph's contents.

The route is priority review at score ≥ 0.60, otherwise standard review. Neither route is verified correct: there are no outcome labels, training, performance estimates, timing checks or actual encoders. A real fallback and its thresholds need independent evaluation.
