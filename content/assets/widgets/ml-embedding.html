Exact training rule and assumptions

For the selected vector e and fixed q = (1, 0), score z = q · e and model probability p = 1/(1 + exp(−z)). Binary log loss is −ln(p) for target 1 and −ln(1 − p) for target 0. Natural logs give nats. Each plain gradient step uses e ← e − (p − target)q with learning rate 1, without regularization or a learned context. Only the selected known row changes. The displayed next-step preview is not applied until a learning button is pressed.

Dot product sums matching coordinate products. Cosine divides that dot product by both lengths. Retrieval ranks known titles by the selected score, descending, breaking ties by ID. Switching metrics does not retrain or normalize stored vectors. Starting points, title names, feedback and context are synthetic. A non-click assumes an observed exposure. No click data is fetched, no text encoder runs, and no calibrated probability, held-out recommendation quality, privacy guarantee or semantic interpretation is established.
