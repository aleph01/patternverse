Assumptions and exact calculations

All ten contexts and potential rewards are fixed synthetic records. A is always preferred. With K eligible actions, the logger assigns A probability 1 − ε + ε/K and every other eligible action ε/K. Excluded actions have probability zero. A draw u in [0,1) selects from the cumulative distribution in A/B/C order, using left-closed, right-open intervals. These fixed illustrative draws make comparisons reproducible; this replay is not a live randomization service or a statistical experiment.

The logger records chosen action, its actual probability, context, eligible actions, decision ID, and policy version v1 before attaching its observed binary reward. All outcomes are immediate and complete; real missing rewards must not be converted to zero. A chosen action can only have positive logging probability.

The target is fixed independently of this log. For a deterministic target, weight w is 1/p on a matching row and zero otherwise. Raw IPS = sum(w × reward)/10. Self-normalized IPS = sum(w × reward)/sum(w), undefined when no rows match. Capped IPS = sum(min(w,cap) × reward)/10; no cap equals raw IPS. Only capped IPS responds to the cap control; the original propensities, raw IPS, SNIPS, and diagnostics remain unchanged.

Support is checked for the target action in every fixture context, using the known logger distribution, before reporting any policy-value estimate. If a target action has probability zero, estimates are blocked even if some rows match. Positive support with zero sampled matches instead yields raw IPS zero, undefined SNIPS, and a warning about absent evidence.

The weight-concentration diagnostic is (sum w)²/sum(w²), or zero with no matches. It is not a confidence interval or a claim about independent effective observations. Raw IPS may exceed one in a finite sample; SNIPS remains within the observed reward range when defined but can be biased. Capping introduces bias. No confidence intervals, policy training, delayed outcomes, adaptive collection, interference, or long-term effects are modeled. No action leaves this example.
