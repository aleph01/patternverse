The fixed rule in this example

Names are trimmed and normalized to Unicode NFC; case is preserved. Each becomes channel=name. FNV-1a hashes its UTF-8 bytes to an unsigned 32-bit integer. The bucket is the remainder after dividing that integer by the bucket count.

This unsigned teaching hasher is not scikit-learn's signed MurmurHash3. Identical rules give identical assignments. All calculations happen locally in this example.
