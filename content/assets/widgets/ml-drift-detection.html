Monitoring assumptions and limits

Reference R always has 100 tickets, with 80 web and 20 app. The mix-change scenario uses 20% web and 80% app in the current window; the other scenarios retain 80% web and 20% app. M1 is a fixed rule, not a trained classifier. Every example ticket has a reviewed label, with no delay or selection bias simulated.

The statistic is the maximum absolute difference between channel shares, reported in percentage points. It is L-infinity distance on the two-category distribution, multiplied by 100. Comparison uses integer count products to preserve the strict threshold boundary. Fewer than 50 current tickets withholds the input-alert decision even though the descriptive gap remains displayed.

Gray bars show the reference and green bars show current data. Green does not indicate good performance. This is a window comparison with a practical threshold, not a hypothesis test, ADWIN implementation, uncertainty interval or false-alarm estimate. No sampling randomness, seasonality, serial dependence, missing or unknown categories, joint-feature detection, model retraining or real deployment is simulated. Invalid records are rejected rather than silently excluded.
