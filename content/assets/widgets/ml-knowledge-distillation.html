Exact objective and update rules

Teacher logits are fixed throughout a run. The student starts at [0, 0, 0]. Every step subtracts 0.5 times the analytic gradient from each student logit. Runs stop at 20 steps. These are free scores for this single example, with no input features or shared neural weights.

At training temperature T, p = softmax(teacher logits / T) and q = softmax(student logits / T). At deployment temperature one, r = softmax(student logits). The true-label index is 0, Delivery.

Hard-label CE = −ln(r[0]). Scaled distillation loss = T² × Σ p[i] ln(p[i] / q[i]). Total = (1 − α) × CE + α × scaled distillation loss. Alpha weights the teacher term. All logarithms are natural; losses are for this one example, with no batch averaging.

The student-logit gradient is (1 − α) × (r − one-hot Delivery) + α × T × (q − p). The teacher receives no updates. A lower teacher mismatch can coexist with a larger loss against the true label. Training scores here say nothing about held-out accuracy or deployment speed.
