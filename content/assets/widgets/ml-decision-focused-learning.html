Exact model, solver, and assumptions

The predictor is [5.5 + θ, 4.5 + θ, 10.5 + 1.5θ]. The fitting grid is θ = 0, 0.1, …, 2.0. MSE is the mean squared error across all three observed benefits. These scores are a deliberately limited family; neither fit is guaranteed to reproduce the observed values.

Jobs require [2, 2, 3] capacity units. Each job is indivisible, benefits add, and not spending all capacity is allowed. The solver enumerates all eight subsets, discards those over budget, and maximizes the sum of predicted benefits. For ties within 0.000000001 it prefers fewer jobs, then alphabetical subset order.

Regret is the best true feasible benefit minus the true benefit of the action selected from predictions. The reference optimum is computed using observed benefits with the same constraints. Observed benefits score candidates during fitting; they are not passed into the predicted-benefit decision rule.

The displayed benefits are complete labels for every job. There are no hidden counterfactuals, uncertain capacities, job interactions, approximate solves, or held-out examples. No neural optimizer, relaxation, or SPO+ surrogate is simulated. Changing an input refits both objectives.
