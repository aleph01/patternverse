Fitting assumptions and limits

All observations have identical recorded features, so the fitted outputs are constants. The mean minimizes squared error. Band counts divided by sample size give the unregularized categorical frequency fit; empty bands have zero fitted mass. Ranges include their left boundary and exclude their right boundary. Supported durations are 0 ≤ duration < 40 minutes.

Bounds describe how much fitted band mass could lie at or beyond the deadline. They are not confidence intervals or guarantees about future data. Uniform within-band interpolation and midpoint reconstruction are optional teaching assumptions, not information supplied by the fitted masses. No feature-dependent classifier, calibration, quantile model or generalization experiment runs.
