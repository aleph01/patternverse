Fit a ridge model to four fictional training records for each attempted penalty. Select the lowest validation MSE among completed trials. The budget limits which settings are tried; no independent test outcomes are supplied.

Inspect records, fits and validation errors

Inputs and responses are dimensionless. Every model predicts wx with intercept fixed at zero. Fitting minimizes the sum of squared training errors plus λw²; selection minimizes mean squared validation error. These are different quantities.

Search assumptions and limits

The two trial orders are fixed teaching sequences, not random or adaptive search. Each attempted setting receives the same training records and its exact scalar fit. The budget counts completed fits, not elapsed time. Settings are 0, 2, 8 and 26; a tie selects the smaller λ regardless of execution order.

Panels A and B are separate constructed validation scenarios, not random resamples or estimates of uncertainty. Their outcomes never enter fitting. No test set, refitting on combined data, preprocessing estimation, cross-validation, Bayesian optimization, early stopping or noisy training is simulated. Zero error on a constructed panel does not establish future performance. Bars use one fixed scale from 0 to 2.5 squared-response units; a dot marks zero, dashes mean untried, and green identifies the selected completed trial.
