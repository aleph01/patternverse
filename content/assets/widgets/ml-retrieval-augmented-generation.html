      Scoring assumptions and limits
      

        Only passage bodies are indexed. Words are lowercase ASCII letter-or-digit sequences matched
        by [a-z0-9]+; punctuation separates words. There is no stemming or stopword removal. Each
        distinct query word contributes once; repeated passage words retain their frequency. Query
        words absent from a passage contribute zero.
      

      

        For term t in passage d: IDF(t) = ln(1 + (6 − df(t) + 0.5)/(df(t) + 0.5)). Its contribution
        is IDF(t) × tf(t,d) × (1.2 + 1) / (tf(t,d) + 1.2 × (1 − 0.75 + 0.75 ×
        length(d)/average_length)). Sum contributions over distinct query terms. The six lengths are
        15, 15, 16, 14, 17, and 14; average = 91/6. Corpus statistics stay fixed across eligibility
        settings.
      

      

        Filter eligibility before taking the highest-scoring k positive-score passages. Public
        readers exclude staff passages; current-only excludes superseded passages. Exact ties use
        ascending passage ID. This is an illustrative BM25 variant, not byte-for-byte Lucene
        scoring. Scores are neither probabilities nor semantic support checks. A common word can
        cause an irrelevant match; no hit does not prove that a policy is absent.
      

      

        There are no embeddings, model calls, generated responses, entailment checks, accuracy
        estimates, or real authorization. The diagram's generator is conceptual. Instructions do not
        enforce security; real permissions must be checked outside the model before content reaches
        it. This fixture uses a fixed fictional current-version snapshot, not live document updates.
