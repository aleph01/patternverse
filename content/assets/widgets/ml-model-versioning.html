Calculation assumptions and limits

Distance is in kilometres. T1 uses mean 10 km and scale 10 km; T2 uses mean 20 km and scale 10 km. The normalized value z has no units. W1 computes 20 + 5 × z minutes; W2 computes 24 + 4 × z minutes. P1 reviews estimates ≥ 30 minutes; P2 reviews estimates ≥ 32 minutes. Equality triggers review. Constants are chosen, not fitted.

The comparison uses the same raw request, exact component IDs, and deterministic formulas. An estimate difference is relative to the requested release, not error against a real delivery outcome. A matching estimate or action cannot establish component identity. Reverting artifacts does not undo earlier actions.
