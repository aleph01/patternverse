      Masking, probability, and stopping assumptions
      

        For each non-EOS token, append its complete string piece to the current prefix. It is legal
        if at least one accepted target starts with that result. EOS contributes no characters and
        is legal only when the current prefix exactly equals an accepted target. Every offered valid
        prefix has a tokenized path to completion in this handcrafted vocabulary.
      

      

        Base probability is weight divided by total weight. Grammar-retained mass is the sum of base
        probabilities of legal tokens. With the mask enabled, illegal tokens get zero and legal
        weights are divided by their sum. If that sum is zero, stop with DEAD_END. With no mask,
        keep the base distribution, including illegal tokens. Greedy choice uses the highest
        effective probability. This is not random sampling, beam search, temperature scaling, top-k
        filtering, or global sequence-conditioned sampling.
      

      

        All ten weights stay constant after every token within a profile. A real language model
        changes its scores with context. The two profiles are invented distributions, not measured
        models. The handcrafted string pieces do not reproduce a real tokenizer's byte behavior.
        Different token paths can produce the same text.
      

      

        The prefilled prefix does not count against the new-token budget. EOS does count as one new
        token. FINISHED means EOS was emitted; TOKEN_LIMIT means the budget was exhausted without
        EOS, even if the text already equals an accepted object. DEAD_END means no
        positive-probability token remains. Accepted text and terminated generation are reported
        separately. No constraint bypass or automatic repair occurs at a dead end.
