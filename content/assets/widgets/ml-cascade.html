Later lists offline supplied answers for every part, including stopped cases. Those answers are not executed on stopped cases in the simulated cascade. One means defect; zero means ordinary.

Assumptions and limits

Continue when score ≥ threshold; equality passes. Stopped parts receive ordinary (zero). Survivors receive their supplied later-stage answer. Gate scores are ranking signals, not calibrated probabilities. All labels are known for evaluation and never determine the gate.

Work is the sum of gate calls times gate cost and later calls times later cost. Costs are constant supplied milliseconds per part. The comparison runs the later model alone on every part, without the gate. Common preprocessing, networking, queues, parallel execution and batching are excluded. Sequential path work is not measured response latency or a throughput estimate.

Recall is detected defects divided by all four reviewed defects. Gate retention is passed defects divided by all defects; conditional later recall is detected defects divided by passed defects. A zero denominator displays N/A. The matching-answer option is deliberately perfect on these eight invented rows, not a claim about an actual detector. No model is trained, called or timed.
