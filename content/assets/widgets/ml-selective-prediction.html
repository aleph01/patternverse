Assumptions and scope

These are fixed synthetic evaluation records, not a fitted model or a statistically valid threshold-selection procedure. All thresholds can be explored for explanation; a deployed threshold needs separate development and evaluation data. Scores are ranking signals, not verified correctness probabilities. Equality is accepted; tied scores move together.

Automation coverage is accepted / all; selective risk is errors / accepted, undefined when none are accepted. Neither is conformal set coverage. Review costs assume every deferred ticket receives a correct human resolution, with no delay cost, and every automatic error costs ten units. The full-review cost is hypothetical when demand exceeds capacity. The explorer does not simulate reviewer errors, waiting times, guarantees, or population uncertainty.
