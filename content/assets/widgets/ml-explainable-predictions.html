Attribution assumptions and limits

The model is fixed and additive, with zero interactions. The two-feature Shapley calculation averages each feature's increment across both addition orders using the declared background-replacement rule. For this model, the exact result is coefficient × (input − reference mean). No SHAP package, surrogate approximation or statistical fitting is run.

Reference S has trips (1 km, off-peak) and (3 km, off-peak). Reference M has (3 km, off-peak) and (5 km, rush hour). These are chosen backgrounds, not representative population samples. Replacement breaks the association between a retained feature and its missing counterpart. A conditional explanation could allocate credit differently; it is not computed here.

Every output and contribution uses minutes. Green adds minutes and dark gray removes minutes; neither color signals model quality. The diagram uses a fixed 0–32 minute scale. The decomposition sums exactly for these inputs, but does not establish prediction accuracy, confidence, fairness, real-world causality or feasible advice. Model M1's four-minute rush term is not an estimated benefit of changing departure time.
