Metric and uncertainty assumptions

No model is trained or called. Each unique row has a verified synthetic label and two preassigned binary predictions. Accuracy is (TP + TN) / rows. Positive recall is TP / (TP + FN); its denominator can be smaller than the row count. Slice membership is determined by the attributes in the records, including crossed and overlapping conditions. Tablet is a declared category with no records. The overall result always includes all 20 rows, irrespective of view and filter.

The interval is the Wilson binomial score interval with z = 1.96: for k successes among n trials, center = (k/n + z²/(2n))/(1 + z²/n) and half-width = z sqrt((k/n)(1 − k/n)/n + z²/(4n²))/(1 + z²/n). Zero denominators show N/A. These illustrative, separate-model intervals assume independent binomial outcomes sampled from a relevant population. The fixed fictional cases do not establish a real population rate.

These are not intervals for the paired A/B difference or simultaneous intervals adjusted for searching slices. Overlapping memberships are not independent observations. There is no causal analysis, demographic fairness certification, threshold optimization, or deployment-population estimate. A row-count display filter is not a statistical reliability threshold.
