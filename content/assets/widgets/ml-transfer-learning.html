Assumptions and calculation

Inputs are (1,0), (−1,0), (0,1), (0,−1). All quantities are dimensionless. Encoder h = a x₁ + b x₂; prediction = w h; no biases, regularization, or randomness. Loss is the sum of squared prediction errors divided by eight: half mean squared error over four rows. For each row let r = prediction − target. Average r w x₁, r w x₂, and r h over the four rows to obtain the gradients for a, b, and w. Subtract 0.5 times each permitted gradient simultaneously. Calculations use full precision; labels round.

No source dataset is fitted, no training-from-scratch baseline is simulated, and no held-out score is measured. Lower training loss does not establish positive transfer. Reset also closes optional details.
