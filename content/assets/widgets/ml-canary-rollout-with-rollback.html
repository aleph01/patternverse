Request Serving Quality
Assumptions and exact rules

Each window has 100 distinct requests with buckets 0–99. A bucket below the current percentage receives the candidate response; all others receive the incumbent. These deterministic buckets illustrate routing, not randomized causal inference. The schedule is 10 → 25 → 50 → 100%, or 25 → 50 → 100%. Windows have equal request counts, not measured durations.

Incumbent serving failures occur at request positions 50 and 100. Otherwise, incumbent predictions are wrong at positions divisible by 20. The healthy candidate has no serving failures and is wrong at positions divisible by 20. Serving-failure behavior fails positions 3, 13, 23, … from window 2 onward. Quality-regression behavior instead makes predictions wrong at positions divisible by 5 from window 2 onward. Truth is position modulo 2; a wrong prediction flips that binary label. All outcomes are synthetic.

At each boundary: roll back if candidate serving failures exceed 5% of candidate requests, or observed prediction errors exceed 10% of labeled successful candidate responses. Otherwise hold if candidate requests are below the chosen minimum, or quality labels are missing. Otherwise advance; a passing 100% window promotes. Equality passes. A known breach takes priority over insufficient evidence. These mechanical gates perform no significance test.

Labels, when ready, cover every successful response. Serving failures have no prediction and never count as correct predictions. Zero labeled outcomes have an undefined quality rate. Lag adds one full window at the failing share, using the same fault rules, before routing future requests to the incumbent. No lag is added for a hold or promotion.

A hold ends this replay at its first inconclusive gate; a real hold would keep exposing traffic unless stopped separately. Rollback is assumed to succeed with warm, compatible incumbent capacity. There are no retries, in-flight requests, shared-state effects, capacity limits, or automatic repairs. Exposure is requests, not unique people or a measure of harm. No ML model is trained here.
