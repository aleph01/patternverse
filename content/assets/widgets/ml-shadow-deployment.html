Assumptions and scope

Requests arrive every 25 ms, starting at zero. Incumbent latency alternates 20 and 25 ms, with unlimited independent incumbent capacity. Shadow workers are identical, non-preemptive, and take the fixed service durations listed in the records. Copies use an unbounded FIFO queue; ties choose the lowest numbered worker. End-to-end shadow latency includes queue wait. Equality at the deadline is accepted.

A deadline miss makes a result unusable for the collector but does not cancel queued or running work. All scheduled work eventually runs. Worker-milliseconds sum shadow service durations and are not a monetary cost or an end-to-end latency. No retries, network, batching, logging failures, crashes, or resource contention are simulated. Real deployments need bounded admission and isolation; the assumed unbounded queue is a teaching simplification.

Odd-ID and three-ID selections are fixed illustrative subsets, not random samples. Partial labels hide R08 and R10. A labeled pair requires both an on-time shadow result and an available label; disagreement needs no label. Empty denominators are undefined. No user receives a shadow prediction, and the fixed incumbent latency remains unchanged only because complete isolation is assumed.

This local replay sends no requests, trains no model, and estimates no business effect. Shadow inference, tools, and logging in a real system must have production side effects disabled.
