Freshness assumptions and limits

The source has observation coverage from 08:00 through 10:20 for A and C. Available rows are the operational input, not eventual corrected truth. Events are unique; no corrections, duplicates, source outages or retractions are simulated. Refreshes publish instantaneously on UTC clock multiples of 5 or 15 minutes, or remain paused at 09:45. Each simulated snapshot is a complete available count at its refresh cutoff.

Model M1 is pinned to v1. The guard requires matching definition identity, a present value and age between 0 and 10 minutes inclusive. The age is serving time minus snapshot cutoff, not time since the last order. Rejected inputs require a declared fallback; this example supplies no model or substitute value. Storage TTL, historical lookback and this consumer age check are separate concepts.

This is original teaching code, not a Feast client or benchmark. It computes windows and a latest-value copy in memory. It does not operate a registry, measure latency, train a model or establish predictive accuracy. Sharing code in this example does not prove that separate production engines agree.
