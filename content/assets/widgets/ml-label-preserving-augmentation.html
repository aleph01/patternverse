The same transformation can preserve one target and change another. This explorer transforms an actual 8 × 8 array; its targets come from exact synthetic templates, not a trained model.

Validity refers only to the selected synthetic target. Positive contrast is detectable exactly in this toy setting; camera noise, perceptual limits, and predictive accuracy are not modeled.

Exact transformation and target rules

The source values are 100 on the arrow and 0 on the background. A horizontal flip reverses column order; a vertical flip reverses row order. Contrast multiplies every value by contrast / 100, with no rounding or clipping.

After ignoring positive intensity, the target checker compares the entire array with the exact right and left templates. For presence, either template is “Arrow present” and the blank array is “No arrow”. For direction, the templates are Right and Left; a blank array has undefined direction. Other masks are outside this toy domain.

The four possible flip settings are recomputed at the current contrast and deduplicated by their full pixel arrays. A vertically symmetric source means four settings need not create four distinct images. These are views of one original, not independent observations.
