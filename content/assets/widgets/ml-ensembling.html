MAE averages absolute errors. RMSE first averages squared errors and then takes the square root; it is also measured in orders. Both summaries use these same six fictional outcomes. No fitted parameters or model-selection data are hidden behind the controls.

Assumptions and optional detail

Actual orders are 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60. Complementary signed errors are A (6, −6, 0, 6, −6, 0), B (0, 6, −6, 0, 6, −6), C (−6, 0, 6, −6, 0, 6). In the shared scenario all three use A's errors. In the poor-member scenario A and B have opposing ±2 errors, while C always has error +30. Each forecast is the fictional actual plus its stipulated error. This construction is for explanation, not a predictor allowed to access outcomes in deployment.

The equal mean adds forecasts and divides by three. The weighted mean computes (2A + B + C)/4; its nonnegative weights are fixed, not learned here. Median sorts three forecasts and takes the middle one. Neither method selects a member using the actual outcome. Fractional orders are valid numerical forecasts; no rounding or clipping is applied before evaluation.
