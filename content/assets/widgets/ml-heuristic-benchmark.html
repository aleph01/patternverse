Assumptions and exact calculations

Accuracy = correct predictions / 20. Assigned batch cost = missed late deliveries × selected penalty + false alerts × 1. Both policies predict every row; there are no failed predictions or missing labels. Bars share a cost scale whose upper limit is the larger displayed cost or 10, whichever is greater. The green segment is missed-delivery cost and the gray segment is false-alert cost; green does not indicate success.

The always-on-time reference and the 10-minute rule are fixed in advance. The candidate scores are invented, not fitted. This is descriptive arithmetic on synthetic records, not a confidence interval, a held-out generalization estimate, or a causal estimate of what alerts achieve. Exploring thresholds on the displayed labels cannot validate a final policy. No external requests, deployment costs, capacity limits or review effects are modeled.
