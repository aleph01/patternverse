The exact learning and evaluation rules

The pool contains integer scores 1–99. The simulator labels a score positive when it is at least the true cutoff. The classifier fits the midpoint between the largest revealed negative score and the smallest revealed positive score, then predicts positive at or above that midpoint.

Uncertainty selection requests the remaining score closest to that midpoint, breaking ties toward the lower score. Random selection samples without replacement using a seeded pseudorandom generator. Both strategies use the same initial labels, model family, and query budget.

Evaluation uses 200 separate scores: 0.25, 0.75, …, 99.75. Neither their labels nor the simulator's true cutoff are passed to selection or fitting. The results describe one noiseless, one-dimensional scenario, not a benchmark for real datasets. Change the seed and scenario to inspect other runs.

The starting labels are free only inside this comparison. A real project must also budget for its initial labels, independent evaluation labels, annotation review, and computation.
