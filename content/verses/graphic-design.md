Graphic design connects a communication purpose with the form a viewer can use. Begin with [[graphic-design-patterns|the Graphic Design problem guide]]: choose what is going wrong, follow a suggested starting pattern, or browse the eight families.

The authored collection contains 39 core patterns across eight active families. Each pattern has a distinct decision; scale, background treatments, silhouette representation, typeface pairing and other methods appear within their parent decisions. A supporting [[graphic-design-foundations-contrast|contrast guide]] points to the appropriate repair.

[[graphic-design-establish-a-shared-visual-language|Establish a Shared Visual Language]] coordinates roles and permitted variation across related artifacts. [[graphic-design-develop-distinctive-visual-cues|Develop Distinctive Visual Cues]] addresses recognition among external alternatives. [[graphic-design-build-reusable-templates|Build Reusable Templates]] makes recurring production reliable when content varies. All 39 planned core decisions now have authored drafts.
