A result is useful when its question, observation process, assumptions, and uncertainty are understood together. Statistics gathers practical patterns for those tasks, with original worked examples and interactive demonstrations.

Begin with [[statistics-field-guide]], choose [[statistics-path-introductory]] or [[statistics-path-analyst]], or investigate a suspicious result through [[statistics-traps]].

The guide connects study design and sampling to estimation, models, dependence, time-to-event analysis, causal inference, evidence synthesis, and decisions. A method is introduced through the recurring problem it addresses and the conditions under which its response is defensible.
