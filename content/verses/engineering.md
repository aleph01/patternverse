Engineering examines how a change in architecture, geometry, materials, timing, or control produces a useful physical result, and what conditions limit that result.

Begin with [[engineering-function-and-architecture|Function and Architecture]]. Its first article, [[engineering-segmentation|Segmentation]], examines where to divide equipment so a local intervention can preserve the useful remainder.

This draft reading edition contains completed articles as they are authored.
