AI Usage gathers patterns for working thoughtfully with AI: framing useful questions, making intentions explicit, exploring alternatives, and examining the limits of plausible answers. The emphasis is on reusable ways of thinking, not particular tools or prompt tricks.

Start with [[thinking-with-language-models|Thinking with Language Models]], a collection of six approaches for turning an agreeable assistant into a more useful thinking partner. Model-generated suggestions and critiques are aids to judgment, not substitutes for evidence, tests, or independent review.
