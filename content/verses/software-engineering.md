Software Engineering explores how software is structured and how its parts collaborate. Begin with [[gof-design-patterns|GoF Design Patterns]]: 23 classic object-oriented designs, explained through concrete examples and their limitations.

[[software-application-architecture|Application Architecture]] examines the wider arrangement of responsibilities and dependencies, beginning with [[layers|Layers]].

[[software-domain-modeling-business-logic|Domain Modeling and Business Logic]] examines how business concepts and rules become executable behavior, beginning with [[domain-model|Domain Model]].

[[software-persistence-transactions|Persistence and Transactions]] examines domain-facing data access and the responsibilities behind persistence, beginning with [[repository|Repository]].

[[software-presentation-ui-state|Presentation and UI State]] examines input, display, and state synchronization, beginning with [[model-view-controller|Model-View-Controller]].

[[software-messaging-integration|Messaging and Integration]] examines message contracts and independent delivery paths, beginning with [[data-publish-subscribe-distribution|Publish-Subscribe]].

[[software-distributed-data-coordination|Distributed Data and Coordination]] examines operations that cross independent consistency boundaries, beginning with [[saga|Saga]].

[[software-concurrency-resource-management|Concurrency and Resource Management]] examines waiting, execution, and resource lifetimes, beginning with [[reactor|Reactor]].

[[software-reliability-failure-handling|Reliability and Failure Handling]] examines bounded waiting and explicit failure outcomes, beginning with [[timeout|Timeout]].

[[software-performance-scalability|Performance and Scalability]] examines repeated work and growth, beginning with [[cache-aside|Cache-Aside]].

[[software-security-access-boundaries|Security and Access Boundaries]] examines authorized access and protected enforcement, beginning with [[reference-monitor|Reference Monitor]].

[[software-testing-testability|Testing and Testability]] examines controlled scenarios and useful observations, beginning with [[test-double|Test Double]].

[[software-evolution-delivery|Evolution and Delivery]] examines compatibility and controlled transitions in running systems, beginning with [[data-incremental-replacement|Strangler Fig]].
