**Archived course draft · 30 October 2026.** The songwriting course linked below has been set aside while a new musical-composition course is designed.

Music connects rhythm, pitch, language, performance, and sound through time. These patterns focus on recurring songwriting situations and useful responses, with concrete comparisons that can help an existing draft.

Begin with [[songwriting-course|From First Idea to Finished Song]], 49 patterns in an eight-part guided sequence or a problem-led field guide. The [[songwriting-example-songbook|original songbook]] follows two contrasting examples, and the [[songwriting-listening-guide|listening guide]] connects the ideas to official artist sources.

New to notes, scales, keys, and chords? The first five patterns in [[songwriting-part-3|Notes, Keys, and Chords]] build those relationships step by step, with short playable comparisons.

To follow ideas across a whole piece, begin with [[songwriting-choose-a-song-form|Choose a Form That Serves the Song]]. It connects sectional returns, transformed material and process-driven development. [[songwriting-shape-energy-over-time|Shape Energy over Time]] adds an original whole-piece comparison: a repeating figure with constant texture or gradually entering layers.

This collection is an unpublished local draft for review. Its articles contain worked examples and static diagrams. Selected original examples also have a piano-roll player with playback and part controls; this edition has no interactive practices.
