Architecture concerns the places people inhabit: ground, streets, buildings, rooms and details. Explore 36 connected field guides with real architectural precedents and original explanatory drawings.

Open the [[architecture-field-guide|Architecture Field Guide]], or browse by relationship:

- [[architecture-place|Place]] — site, public realm and building form.
- [[architecture-space|Space]] — organization, movement and lived experience.
- [[architecture-fabric|Fabric]] — comfort, construction and longevity.
- [[architecture-practice|Practice]] — observation, alternatives, tests and feedback.

The guides preserve established authorship and distinguish modern synthesis. They are educational tools for understanding design choices; project requirements need the appropriate local and professional assessment.
