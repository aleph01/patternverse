Problem Solving collects ways to turn a concern into an investigation and a workable response. [[structure-a-problem]] separates possible causes, answerable questions, supported constraints, and inherited conventions.

Use [[reframing]] when the problem itself needs reconsideration, and [[getting-unstuck]] when you need meaningfully different possibilities. [[understand-systems]] helps when relationships and feedback over time explain more than a single isolated cause.
