Communication & Relationships explores how people listen, express needs, respond to one another, and develop recurring ways of relating. It brings together approaches to understanding interactions and choosing responses that support clearer communication and workable relationships.

Start with [[nonviolent-communication|Nonviolent Communication]] for eight guides to clearer expression, listening, requests, and appreciation. Explore [[games-people-play|Games People Play]] for 36 illustrated articles on recurring exchanges, or [[bowen-family-systems|Bowen Family Systems Theory]] for six guides to relationship pressure, reciprocal responses, family patterns, and changing your own participation. The articles include context, worked examples, and limits, with illustrations where they help explain the interaction. The verse also makes room for assertiveness, boundaries, conflict, and repair.

Their relationship to psychological change also connects this verse with [[therapy|Therapy & Wellbeing]], while communication in shared work connects it with [[collaboration|Collaboration]].
