Writing turns observations, ideas, and imagined experience into something a reader can follow.
These patterns help you choose what to include, how to arrange it, and how language can serve
the effect you intend.

Begin with [[writing-patterns|Writing Patterns]], a collection of 54 techniques in nine families:

- [[writing-clarity|Clarity and orientation]]
- [[writing-precision|Precision and economy]]
- [[writing-concrete-experience|Concrete experience]]
- [[writing-rhythm|Rhythm and emphasis]]
- [[writing-voice|Voice, perspective, and dialogue]]
- [[writing-narrative-movement|Narrative movement]]
- [[writing-structure|Structure and coherence]]
- [[writing-argument|Argument and persuasion]]
- [[writing-drafting|Drafting and revision]]

Choose a technique for the problem in front of you. Its usefulness depends on the material,
the reader, and what the piece is trying to do.
