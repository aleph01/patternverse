*Use the wrong similar-sounding word as an apparent mistake.*

A speaker reaches confidently for an impressive word and lands on a similar-sounding wrong one. The error carries two effects: an unexpected meaning and a glimpse of the speaker’s misplaced confidence.

## Example 1 · Norm Crosby

A tribute to Wilt Chamberlain

In a tribute to Wilt Chamberlain, Crosby substitutes insulation for inspiration and standing ovulation for standing ovation. He keeps the sound and manners of sincere praise while the literal meaning becomes absurd.

— Norm Crosby

**The turn:** The audience recognizes the intended praise underneath the verbal mistakes.

[Source](<https://www.seniorvoicealaska.com/story/2017/06/01/columns/norm-crosby-forever-the-malaprop-master/1390.html>)

## Example 2 · Norm Crosby

A confidently mangled history lesson

Crosby’s lecturer sends the Trojans against Eucalyptians, drives the defeated out of Trojia, then ends on the Connecticut Turnpike. The narrator maintains scholarly confidence while names and geography fall apart.

— Norm Crosby

**The turn:** The mock lecture mixes verbal misuse, invented names and an anachronistic highway. Only substitutions with a recoverable intended word clearly teach malapropism. The highway supplies a separate historical category break; a wrong proper name alone does not establish a sound-alike error.

[Source](<https://eta.creativecirclecdn.com/stonecounty/files/20201120-130518-Stone_County_Leader_12_31_2014.pdf>)

## Example 3 · Norm Crosby

A mock historical medical report

A mock historical account of Brutus being stabbed supplies a rusty sieve as the weapon and calls a medical consequence rigor motion. The solemn report is undermined by its bizarre vocabulary.

— Norm Crosby

**The turn:** Rigor motion points recognizably toward rigor mortis, making that substitution a clear malapropism. The rusty sieve supplies another absurd image, but its intended replacement is not established by this description. The two oddities should not be treated as equally demonstrated verbal errors.

[Source](<https://eta.creativecirclecdn.com/stonecounty/files/20201120-130518-Stone_County_Leader_12_31_2014.pdf>)

## What makes the structure work

The intended word should be recoverable. Crosby keeps the surrounding sentence authoritative, which makes the wrong word more conspicuous. Some passages also use invented proper names or absurd history; those are neighboring devices rather than pure malapropisms.
