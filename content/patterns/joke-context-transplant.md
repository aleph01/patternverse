*Make the rules, habits or characters of one setting operate in another.*

Take a person, rule or story-world into a setting whose expectations do not accommodate it. The new setting makes a familiar role operate under unfamiliar constraints. Status and genre become especially easy to disturb.

This is a descriptive working label for the comic mechanism.

## Example 1 · John Mulaney

A horse loose in a hospital — Kid Gorgeous

To describe the Trump presidency, Mulaney imagines a horse roaming a hospital. Staff, spectators and commentators respond through ordinary institutional procedures, although the intruder cannot understand the place it has entered. The invented setting supports news updates, reactions and escalating threats throughout the bit.

— John Mulaney

## Example 2 · Steven Wright

Fortune-telling cards used for a poker game

> I stayed up all night playing poker with Tarot cards. I got a full house and four people died.  

— Steven Wright

**The turn:** The rules of divination keep operating inside a card game: a poker result also produces a deadly prediction. Two normally separate systems are made to govern the same hand.

[Source](<https://snltranscripts.jt.org/84/84pwright.phtml>)

## Example 3 · Jerry Seinfeld

A massage parlour as entertainment for the whole family

> Nothing in life is fun for the whole family. There are no massage parlors with ice cream and free jewelry.  

— Jerry Seinfeld

**The turn:** The proposed business combines pleasures associated with different family members. Juxtaposition is central: their incompatible preferences collide. Context Transplant is a secondary reading of the imagined hybrid, since the joke does not develop a scene in which borrowed rules operate.

[Source](<https://www.dead-frog.com/comedians/jokes/jerry-seinfeld>)

## Example 4 · Eddie Izzard

Religious persecution meets tea service — Dress to Kill

Izzard imagines religious persecution conducted in the manners of a Church of England tea service. Officials handle refreshments and execution as though they were choices offered at a counter. Familiar habits of polite service remain in force inside a murderous scenario.

— Eddie Izzard

## Example 5 · Rowan Atkinson

Hell as an induction meeting — live stage monologue

Atkinson’s devil receives the newly dead like an administrator running an induction. He gives practical instructions, sorts arrivals into groups and treats eternal punishment as an organizational task. Hell remains the destination, but the host applies the voice and priorities of an officious meeting leader.

— Rowan Atkinson

## What makes the structure work

Keep the borrowed element recognizable, then follow the destination’s rules consistently enough to generate consequences. A reference alone does little. This working label overlaps with analogy when the new setting also explains the original.
