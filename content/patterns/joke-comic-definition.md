*Redefine something through a surprising description that captures a recognizable quality.*

A familiar thing receives a new definition that captures an inconvenient property, hidden purpose or social truth. The definition is funny because it can feel practically accurate while being formally inappropriate.

## Example 1 · Demetri Martin

Defining glitter by its persistence

> Glitter is the herpes of craft supplies.  

— Demetri Martin

**The turn:** The comparison defines glitter through stubborn persistence.

[Source](<https://www.elle.com/culture/movies-tv/reviews/a9622/whats-so-funny-284646/>)

## Example 2 · Mitch Hedberg

What handing someone a flyer amounts to

> Whenever I walk people try and hand me a flyer. And when someone tries to hand me out a flyer, it's kinda like they're saying, "Here, you throw this away."  

— Mitch Hedberg

**The turn:** The definition exposes the transaction’s likely outcome.

[Source](<https://www.dead-frog.com/comedians/jokes/mitch-hedberg>)

## Example 3 · George Carlin

Translating a polite food preference

> I was what they call a "fussy eater." 'He's fussy! He's a fussy eater!' "Fussy eater" is a euphemism for "Big pain in the ass."  

— George Carlin

**The turn:** A polite category receives a blunt functional definition.

[Source](<https://www.dead-frog.com/comedians/jokes/george-carlin>)

## What makes the structure work

Look for a property the ordinary label politely ignores. A useful definition changes how readers see the object; adding a rude adjective alone seldom produces that shift. Extended definitions can become analogies.
