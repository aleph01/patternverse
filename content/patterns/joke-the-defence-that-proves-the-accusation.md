*Reject a criticism using a defence that demonstrates it.*

A character tries to refute a discreditable interpretation, but the explanation provides fresh evidence for it. The defence is the exposure: listeners need not accept the character’s preferred account of what it means.

This is a descriptive working label for the comic mechanism.

## Example 1 · Jimmy Carr

A defence against sexism becomes another patronizing remark

After sexist material, Carr offers irony and post-modernism as his defence. He then addresses an audience member with a patronizing reassurance about her pretty little head. His stage persona tries to dismiss a criticism while supplying another example of the attitude being criticized.

— Jimmy Carr

**The turn:** The persona’s defence becomes another patronizing remark, demonstrating the very attitude it is meant to dismiss. The comic contradiction belongs to the performed argument; it does not establish Carr’s private beliefs or rule out satire.

## Example 2 · Jimmy Carr

Denying sexism

> I’m not being sexist. How could I be? Some of my best friends are slags.  

— Jimmy Carr

**The turn:** The supposed evidence of respect uses a derogatory category for women.

[Source](<https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/movie_script.php?movie=jimmy-carr-laughing-and-joking>)

## Example 3 · Ron White

Drunk in public — I Got Thrown Out of a Bar

White disputes a public-intoxication accusation by explaining that he had been drinking inside a bar until its staff threw him outside. His defence confirms the drunkenness while arguing about who caused the location.

— Ron White

**The turn:** The explanation concedes intoxication and a public location while disputing who caused the latter. It is a self-defeating defence against the charge’s facts, but potentially a coherent attempt to shift responsibility. This is a boundary case, not proof that every excuse demonstrates the very flaw being denied.

[Source](<https://tatersalad.com/video/official-ron-white-i-got-thrown-out-of-a-bar/>)

## What makes the structure work

Keep the link precise. The defence must demonstrate the trait at issue, rather than merely reveal some unrelated flaw. In persona comedy, distinguish the character’s oblivious argument from the performer’s own beliefs.
