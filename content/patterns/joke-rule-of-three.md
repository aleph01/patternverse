*Establish a pattern with two elements, then exploit the third.*

Two items teach the audience how a list is organized; the third changes its category, scale or perspective. The small amount of repetition creates enough expectation for a compact departure.

## Example 1 · Trevor Noah

American dominance in several categories

> Does America really need to be the best at everything? You already dominate the world in economics, military power and obesity.  

— Trevor Noah

**The turn:** Two prestigious categories establish an expectation the third breaks.

[Source](<https://officialjimmycarr.substack.com/p/problems-problems-problems-i-see>)

## Example 2 · Jim Gaffigan

A Japanese toilet’s escalating features

> The Japanese toilet: it washes you, dries you, does your taxes.  

— Jim Gaffigan

**The turn:** A third function exceeds the appliance’s plausible category.

[Source](<https://officialjimmycarr.substack.com/p/problems-problems-problems-i-see>)

## Example 3 · Rita Rudner

Castles in the air, introduced as a neurotic’s construction

> Neurotics build castles in the air. Psychotics live in them. My mother cleans them.  

— Rita Rudner

**The turn:** The third role shifts the organizing frame.

[Source](<https://officialjimmycarr.substack.com/p/problems-problems-problems-i-see>)

## What makes the structure work

The third item needs a reason to feel different. Three unrelated objects do not automatically form this structure. A longer list can use the same principle, but then rhythm and the point of departure become different decisions.
