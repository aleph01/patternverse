*Follow a punchline with additional punchlines that require no new setup.*

A punchline establishes a comic situation with more possibilities inside it. Tags take further payoffs from that situation without asking the audience to learn a whole new setup. A sequence can change viewpoint, consequence or classification.

## Example 1 · Brian Regan

A science project that is only a cup of dirt

Regan’s schoolboy brings a paper cup of dirt as his science project. The first payoff is his willingness to accept a failing grade and leave. Asked to explain the project, he merely restates the container and its contents, gives that description as its title, then urges the teacher to move along.

— Brian Regan

## Example 2 · Jim Gaffigan

Bacon changes the point of eating a salad

After establishing bacon’s power to improve food, Gaffigan treats a bacon-topped salad as a search for the meat hidden among the leaves. He develops that into a prospecting scene, then compares pretending this meal is healthy to exercising while smoking.

— Jim Gaffigan

## Example 3 · Mitch Hedberg

A cute infestation supports successive payoffs

> My apartment is infested with koala bears. It's the cutest infestation ever. Way better than cockroaches. When I turn on the light, a bunch of koala bears scatter. And I don't want them too. I'm like, "Hey… Hold on fellas… Let me hold one of you and feed you a leaf."  

— Mitch Hedberg

**The turn:** After the first turn makes an infestation cute, the cockroach comparison, the scattering koalas and the invitation to hold and feed one keep paying off the same situation. The last line reverses the normal aim of pest control. This supplied passage also appears under Hyperbole.

[Source](<https://www.dead-frog.com/comedians/jokes/mitch-hedberg>) · [Read the Strategic Grill Locations transcription](<https://temporaryehliss.blogspot.com/2015/04/mitchhedberg-transcript.html>)

## What makes the structure work

An extra line should contribute a new movement, not explain the laugh that just happened. A lengthy new scene may begin another joke. Escalation is one direction for tags, but a useful tag can also move sideways.
