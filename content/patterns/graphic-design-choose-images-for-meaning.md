A beginner handbuilding workshop shows a glazed cup and saucer. The photograph matches ceramics, but says more about owning finished tableware than joining the class.

Choose an image for its specific contribution: activity, feature, identity, setting or atmosphere. Topic and polish do not establish relevance. Inspect what is actually visible and distinguish illustration from evidence of a real service.

## What to notice

After, the same image window shows hands joining a clay coil to an unfinished bowl, with tools and spare coils nearby.](../assets/graphic-design/image-meaning-handbuilding.svg "Only the photograph changes. [Full-size comparison](../assets/graphic-design/image-meaning-handbuilding.svg) · [Stacked narrow-screen view](../assets/graphic-design/image-meaning-handbuilding-mobile.svg)

Replace the tableware photograph with hands joining a clay coil to an unfinished bowl. Keep the image frame, full-image fitting, text and £28 offer unchanged.

The advertised method becomes visible without a new headline. This removes an inference between finished product and participation, but sacrifices lifestyle polish. The original could suit a tableware collection or aspirational campaign. Neither generated photograph documents a class, proves a beginner’s outcome or supplies a complete instructional sequence.

The Australian Government Style Manual recommends relevant images that support content. Here the proposed benefit is an editorial judgment: different subject matter gives readers different information, not a measured performance gain.

## Two variations

### Show the feature the booking depends on

![Two matching Room for two cards keep the same enclosed-room offer, capacity, hourly price and opening hours. Before, the photograph shows open shared desks and several office chairs. After, the same frame shows a table with two chairs in a small room with walls and a closed glass door.](../assets/graphic-design/image-meaning-enclosed-room.svg "Only the photograph changes, from shared workspace to enclosed two-chair room. Layout stays fixed; the image establishes neither sound isolation nor availability.")

[Full-size comparison](../assets/graphic-design/image-meaning-enclosed-room.svg) · [Stacked narrow-screen view](../assets/graphic-design/image-meaning-enclosed-room-mobile.svg)

An enclosed-room offer uses a shared workspace photograph. Select a room showing a table, two chairs, walls and a closed door, preserving the card and showing both full photographs rather than improving one through cropping.

The image supports “enclosed room” and “up to 2 people” more directly, though it loses expansive atmosphere. Walls and a door do not establish acoustic privacy, accessibility or availability. A real page needs verified photographs of the offered room and accurate specifications. GOV.UK’s service-image guidance informs the relevance principle within its government context.

### Match the subject rather than a keyword

![Two matching newsletter pages titled Inside the seed library retain all copy and layout. Before, the photograph shows bookshelves, a reading table and laptop. After, the same image window shows bean seeds, blank paper envelopes, a pencil and a collection tray on a potting bench.](../assets/graphic-design/image-meaning-seed-library.svg "Only the photograph changes, from book-library interior to seeds and packets. Full-image fitting, frame, copy and typography stay fixed.")

[Full-size comparison](../assets/graphic-design/image-meaning-seed-library.svg) · [Stacked narrow-screen view](../assets/graphic-design/image-meaning-seed-library-mobile.svg)

A seed-library introduction shows bookshelves and a laptop. Replace only the photograph with seeds and paper packets. The text’s sharing-and-growing subject now meets relevant objects instead of redirecting a book-borrowing association.

Blank envelopes identify neither varieties nor planting instructions. The replacement loses venue context; an interior could be preferable when the page’s job is finding the location. Neither generated scene records an actual collection or its rules.

## Useful when

Review selection when an attractive image leaves the key question unanswered, repeats a broad category or contradicts the offer. Ask what the viewer should learn from visible content and what still requires words, another view or a diagram.

Selection changes subject, lighting and internal composition together. These pairs hold the surrounding design fixed; they do not isolate each photographic property. Full-image fitting also differs from choosing a favorable crop of the same source.

## Watch out for

A noun match is not a meaning match. A finished cup can be excellent for one brief and weak for another. Mood, metaphor and aspiration are legitimate jobs; literal depiction is not mandatory.

Avoid treating pictures as proof of more than they show. Documentary use requires accurate captions and context, as WBUR emphasizes. If a process needs steps, a diagram may serve it better; if imagery adds no information or useful atmosphere, omission can be appropriate.
