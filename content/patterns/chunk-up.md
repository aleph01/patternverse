### The purpose behind a particular solution

Two colleagues disagree about a report. One wants a dashboard that updates continuously; the other wants a carefully reviewed document each month. Each treats their preferred format as a condition of doing the work properly. When they explain what the format is supposed to achieve, a broader purpose becomes visible: giving decision makers a reliable account of what is happening. The disagreement remains, but it is now possible to compare the formats against that purpose or combine elements of both.

In Sleight of Mouth, Robert Dilts calls the movement from a particular element to a more general category Chunk Up. A dashboard and a monthly report can both be understood as ways of providing visibility. A difficult conversation can be understood as a form of negotiation. A habit can be understood as one strategy for meeting a need. The new category changes the relationships that are available for consideration, because the original example becomes one member of a larger family.

This movement is different from widening the time horizon or considering more people. Those changes alter the frame around an event. Chunk Up changes the level of abstraction at which an element is understood. Looking at a year of reporting changes the time frame; understanding reporting as coordination changes the category. Both may be helpful, but they answer different questions and can reveal different possibilities.

### Recovering alternatives through abstraction

A writer insists that serious writing requires an uninterrupted morning. On days with appointments, nothing gets written. The requirement may be grounded in experience: mornings have often provided energy, privacy, and enough time to settle into the work. Moving upward from an uninterrupted morning to sustained attention reveals what the schedule was supplying. Other arrangements become candidates, such as a quiet evening, a protected hour, or a short session devoted to a task that requires less preparation.

The abstraction does not establish that these alternatives will work. It clarifies what needs testing. If the morning also provides a distinctive quality of thought, then sustained attention may be too broad a category to capture its value. The writer can refine the category rather than abandoning the inquiry. Chunk Up is productive when the broader description preserves the feature that actually matters and makes several concrete ways of providing it imaginable.

The same move can make a personal setback less total. Someone who did poorly in one course may treat the result as proof that education is beyond them. Understanding the course as one learning environment brings other environments into view: self-paced study, small seminars, practical projects, or different forms of assessment. The result from the original course remains relevant. It is now evidence about one arrangement within learning, rather than a verdict on every arrangement that could reasonably bear that name.

### An abstraction can lose the difficulty

Broadening a category can make almost any disagreement appear soluble if enough detail is removed. Both colleagues want reliable information, but they may still face incompatible constraints around cost, speed, review, and access. Describing their aims at a higher level creates a basis for discussion; it does not remove the need to resolve those constraints. A general agreement that cannot guide a specific choice has limited practical value.

This matters in conflict. Saying that two people both want happiness may be accurate and almost useless. A more informative level might be that both want predictability in a shared household, although one seeks it through fixed plans and the other through freedom to adapt. The broader category should be close enough to the disagreement to illuminate it. Abstraction that floats too far above the particulars can sound generous while leaving the original problem untouched.

Another risk is replacing the person's meaning with a preferred theory. A listener might assume that every request is really about security or belonging and continually move the conversation into those terms. Yet the person may have a specific practical concern that requires a practical response. The broader category is a proposal, and its usefulness depends on whether it fits. It should allow the original concern to be described more fully rather than making it disappear into an all-purpose explanation.

### Coming back with a better comparison

Chunk Up is useful when a specific method has become confused with the only legitimate way of achieving something. Find the Positive Intention can help identify the purpose the method serves, while Try Another Model of the World can reveal how another person organizes the larger category. Chunk Down brings the analysis back toward the details needed to judge any alternative. The upward movement provides possibilities; the downward movement checks whether they survive contact with the situation.

The report discussion might end with a continuously updated dashboard and a monthly review of its interpretation. The writer might keep mornings for demanding work and use shorter sessions for revision. These solutions are not guaranteed products of abstraction. They become possible because a concrete arrangement has been understood as a way of achieving a purpose, and therefore as something that can be compared with other arrangements. The category gives the conversation room without relieving it of its responsibilities.

## Pattern details

### Use when

- A preferred method has become the only imaginable solution.
- A specific result is being treated as a verdict on a whole activity.

### Goals

- Recover alternative ways to achieve a purpose.
- Find a useful category that connects opposing proposals.

### Mechanisms

- abstraction
- category expansion

### Not ideal when

- The broader category removes constraints essential to the actual decision.

### Difficulty

easy
