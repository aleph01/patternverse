Two peer workshop names have slightly different sizes. Prices and room labels drift too, suggesting distinctions the programme does not intend.

Choose a manageable set of sizes, assign recurring roles and repeat those assignments. A type scale is a reusable vocabulary, not a fresh decision for every box. Start with a comfortable reading size; give other roles only the separation they need.

## What to notice

![Two identical photographic workshop programmes show Clay bowl and Print a tote. Before, eight font sizes vary between equivalent activity names, times, prices and supporting details. After, four sizes map consistently to page title, activity heading, key facts and supporting labels; all positions, copy, font, weight and imagery remain fixed.](../assets/graphic-design/type-scale-repeated-roles.svg "Only eleven size values change. Eight sizes become four roles: title 40, activity heading 28, facts 22, supporting text 20.")

[Full-size comparison](../assets/graphic-design/type-scale-repeated-roles.svg) · [Stacked narrow-screen view](../assets/graphic-design/type-scale-repeated-roles-mobile.svg)

Replace eight sizes with four role assignments: page title 40, activity headings 28, times/prices 22, supporting labels 20. Keep every baseline, anchor, font, weight and line break fixed.

Equivalent roles now return at equivalent sizes. The intervention repairs the system rather than featuring one session. If a longer title later fails to fit, allow wrapping or revise the component instead of shrinking that item arbitrarily. A genuinely featured session can have a documented exception.

These values are artwork units. They demonstrate assignments, not universally suitable print or CSS sizes. GOV.UK uses size and line-height sets underpinning reusable styles; production styles also need weight and other settings.

## Two variations

### Separate a page subject from its sections

![Two identical photographic annual-review pages show A year of making above Workshops and Open studios. Before, those headings use sizes 31, 30 and 29. After, the page title uses 40 and both section headings use 28; body text, metadata, baselines, photograph and presentation remain unchanged.](../assets/graphic-design/type-scale-distinct-levels.svg "Only headings change: page title 31→40 and sections 30/29→28. Body 22 and supporting text 20 retain the four-step scale.")

[Full-size comparison](../assets/graphic-design/type-scale-distinct-levels.svg) · [Stacked narrow-screen view](../assets/graphic-design/type-scale-distinct-levels-mobile.svg)

Reuse title 40 and section heading 28 in the annual review, preserving body 22 and labels 20. The page heading no longer nearly matches two equivalent section headings. All figures, including 180 workshop places and 420 visitors, stay fixed.

Cross-page reuse makes the vocabulary a scale rather than one isolated enlargement. A dense reference could need a quieter distinction, while three independent panels could appropriately use equal headings.

### Adapt upper steps without shrinking working information

![Two identical phone mockups show Book a day, a day-pass price, workspace photograph, included facilities, booking button and cancellation terms. Before, title and section sizes are 52 and 30. After, they become 36 and 24, while body and button text remain 22 and supporting labels remain 20; all positions and other visual details are unchanged.](../assets/graphic-design/type-scale-phone-roles.svg "Only title and section sizes change, 52→36 and 30→24. Body, button label, terms, anchors and imagery stay fixed.")

[Full-size comparison](../assets/graphic-design/type-scale-phone-roles.svg) · [Stacked narrow-screen view](../assets/graphic-design/type-scale-phone-roles-mobile.svg)

Use title 36 and section heading 24 on the phone booking page; retain body/action text 22 and labels 20. The routine task becomes less display-heavy without shrinking prices or cancellation terms.

A promotional page could justify the larger original. The comparison freezes positions and does not demonstrate reflow; responsive spacing, wrapping and user enlargement still need implementation checks. GOV.UK likewise adapts selected upper steps across screen ranges.

## Useful when

Use a scale when equivalent roles drift or intended levels are nearly indistinguishable. Named styles let repeated decisions change together, while a new page reuses their relationships. Carbon allows contextual type sets rather than one immutable ladder.

Ratios can generate candidates: multiplying 20 by 1.25 gives roughly 20, 25, 31 and 39. That arithmetic is not evidence of better communication. Omit unnecessary steps, revise values and allow roles to share a size when other cues distinguish them.

## Watch out for

Do not collect attractive numbers without assigning jobs or add a size for every minor difference. A mathematically regular ladder can create oversized headings and tiny references. Reader needs take precedence over the specification.

W3C’s Resize Text guidance concerns enlargement without lost content or function. Choosing a scale cannot establish that behavior. Longer content, translations and actual output should test the system rather than be made smaller to protect it.
