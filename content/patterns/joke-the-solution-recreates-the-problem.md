*Introduce a remedy that reproduces the behaviour or condition it was meant to stop.*

A remedy produces the very condition it is meant to remove. The attempted improvement becomes a new source of the trouble. The audience can trace the problem directly to the intervention intended to solve it.

This is a descriptive working label for the comic mechanism.

## Example 1 · Hasan Minhaj

Mental-health apps

> There are over 10,000 apps for mental health. That fact alone gives me anxiety.  

— Hasan Minhaj

**The turn:** The remedy produces its target symptom.

[Source](<https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2020/feb/22/streaming-with-laughter-the-50-best-standup-comedy-shows>)

## Example 2 · Anthony Jeselnik

A gun to protect the children — Thoughts and Prayers

His fictional parents acquire a gun to protect five children, then remove it to protect the four who remain. Their protective intervention has introduced the very danger it was meant to prevent.

— Anthony Jeselnik

**The turn:** The proposed protection becomes the threat.

[Source](<https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/movie_script.php?movie=anthony-jeselnik-thoughts-and-prayers>)

## Example 3 · Rhod Gilbert

Announcements disturb the quiet carriage — official performance clip

Gilbert complains about repeated announcements reminding passengers that they are in a train’s quiet carriage. The announcements ask for quiet while adding another interruption to the space meant to provide it. Enforcing the rule becomes a source of the noise it is supposed to prevent.

— Rhod Gilbert

**The turn:** The intended remedy is a spoken reminder to keep the carriage quiet. Delivering that reminder introduces noise into the same carriage. The remedy itself therefore produces the target problem, rather than merely failing to stop it. Repeated announcements reinforce the contradiction.

[Source](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pk5mI2JPtcc>) · [Read Gilbert’s account of the premise](<https://www.tntmagazine.com/leisure-entertainment/interviews/rhod-gilbert-interview-the-stand-up-comedian-on-being-angry-and-becoming-waless-sexiest-specimen/>) · [Read the contemporary performance review](<https://www.sussexexpress.co.uk/arts-and-culture/theatre-and-stage/review-rhod-gilbert-at-the-dome-892131>)

## What makes the structure work

Name the target problem, identify the remedy and show how using that remedy brings the problem back. A failed remedy, an unrelated side effect or equipment that merely permits risky behaviour does not establish this structure. The comic causal link must be present in the example.
