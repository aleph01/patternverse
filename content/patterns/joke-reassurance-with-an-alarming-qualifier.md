*Offer comfort, then add a restriction that introduces a worse concern.*

A comforting statement arrives with a qualification that removes the comfort. Listeners first recognize the reassuring form, then discover the troubling condition attached to it. A small addition can change the emotional meaning of the whole sentence.

This is a descriptive working label for the comic mechanism.

## Example 1 · Demetri Martin

A small qualifier applied to reassuring statements

> "Sort of" is such a harmless thing to say. But after certain things, "soft of" means everything. Like "I love you" or "You're going to live."  

— Demetri Martin

**The turn:** A tiny qualifier undermines the reassurance.

[Source](<https://www.dead-frog.com/comedians/jokes/demetri-martin>)

## Example 2 · Anthony Jeselnik

A family history of suicide

> I've got a long history of suicide in my family. The good news is it skips a generation. so, if I'm lucky, my kids will kill themselves  

— Anthony Jeselnik

**The turn:** The skipped generation initially offers comfort by exempting the speaker. The ending reveals that his optimism depends on the danger falling on his children instead. The qualification helps set up the turn, but Misplaced Focus is central to the final, self-serving definition of good news.

[Source](<https://www.dead-frog.com/comedians/jokes/anthony-jeselnik>)

## Example 3 · Jackie Mason

Enough money for life—with an impossible condition

> I have enough money to last me the rest of my life, unless I buy something.  

— Jackie Mason

**The turn:** The opening offers recognizable financial reassurance. The unless-clause then makes it conditional on never spending anything, withdrawing the practical security it just promised. The condition itself destroys the comfort; the joke does not merely change whose welfare matters.

[Source](<https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2021/jul/25/jackie-mason-obituary>)

## What makes the structure work

Useful when a persona wants credit for optimism while revealing alarming limits. Keep the reassurance recognizable and the qualification consequential. This is a descriptive working label; the current examples support it more narrowly than a general category of dark jokes.
