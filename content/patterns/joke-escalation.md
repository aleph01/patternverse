*Develop a premise through increasingly extreme examples or consequences.*

An established pattern returns with increasing stakes, specificity, intensity or commitment. Each step uses what listeners already understand while making the situation harder to sustain. The repetition becomes movement.

## Example 1 · Robin Williams

Inventing golf — Live on Broadway

Williams acts out a Scotsman designing golf. Each proposed rule makes the game harder: a distant tiny target, obstacles, awkward equipment, and finally the requirement to repeat the ordeal across eighteen holes.

— Robin Williams

## Example 2 · Jim Gaffigan

Hot Pockets — Beyond the Pale

Gaffigan starts with an ordinary frozen snack, then expands the brand’s cheerful advertising into increasingly unappetizing products and bodily consequences. The recurring jingle keeps endorsing each worse version as if nothing has gone wrong.

— Jim Gaffigan

## Example 3 · Emo Philips

Successive religious-identity tests on the bridge

> Once I saw this guy on a bridge about to jump. I said, "Don't do it!" He said, "Nobody loves me." I said, "God loves you. Do you believe in God?"  
>
> He said, "Yes." I said, "Are you a Christian or a Jew?" He said, "A Christian." I said, "Me, too! Protestant or Catholic?" He said, "Protestant." I said, "Me, too! What franchise?" He said, "Baptist." I said, "Me, too! Northern Baptist or Southern Baptist?" He said, "Northern Baptist." I said, "Me, too! Northern Conservative Baptist or Northern Liberal Baptist?"  
>
> He said, "Northern Conservative Baptist." I said, "Me, too! Northern Conservative Baptist Great Lakes Region, or Northern Conservative Baptist Eastern Region?" He said, "Northern Conservative Baptist Great Lakes Region." I said, "Me, too!"  
>
> "Northern Conservative Baptist Great Lakes Region Council of 1879, or Northern Conservative Baptist Great Lakes Region Council of 1912?" He said, "Northern Conservative Baptist Great Lakes Region Council of 1912." I said, "Die, heretic!" And I pushed him over.  

— Emo Philips

**The turn:** Each agreement creates a narrower test.

[Source](<https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2005/sep/29/comedy.religion>) · [Watch the artist’s archived performance](<https://www.emophilips.com/video/79162850-1F72-4818-A278-458B737C2F08>)

## What makes the structure work

Each new beat should increase an established difficulty, stake, absurdity or degree of specificity. Williams adds impediments, Gaffigan worsens a product’s implications, and Philips keeps narrowing the test for belonging. Merely listing different things does not guarantee escalation.
