The way a situation is framed determines which possibilities are visible.

## Pattern

Before committing to a solution, describe the situation from several genuinely different points of
view. Change the goal, the scale, the assumed constraint, or the person whose needs are foregrounded.

## Example

Instead of asking “How do I finish this perfect first draft?”, ask “What rough material would make
tomorrow's editing easier?”
