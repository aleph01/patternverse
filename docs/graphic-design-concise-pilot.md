# Graphic Design concise article pilot

Revised three contrasting articles on 29 October 2026 using the Data Visualization articles as an editorial reference. This is a pilot, not a verse-wide rewrite.

## Reading structure

A brief problem and principle lead directly into the main visual. One “What to notice” explanation combines diagnosis, intervention and mechanism. Two shorter variations retain different failure modes, followed by practical uses, limits, related patterns and sources.

The repeated context/diagnosis/changes/explanation/tradeoff labels have been removed. Necessary qualifications stay beside the decisions they affect. No interactive widget or site component was added.
