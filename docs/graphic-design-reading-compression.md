# Graphic Design reading compression

Completed locally on 28 October 2026. This pass shortens repeated caption, method and production-credit text across the 39 authored drafts. It does not merge patterns or redesign examples.

## Reading changes

- Reviewed and shortened 120 captions, focusing on the visible intervention, its key controls and any limitation needed to interpret it honestly.
- Removed or condensed repeated pre-example descriptions of unchanged image bytes, crops, typography and presentation.
- Shortened production credits while retaining fictional status, AI-generated photographic provenance, relevant font credits and limits on audience, accessibility, production or identity claims.
- Preserved substantive perceptual reasoning, worked transformations, exceptions, tradeoffs and source-specific qualifications.

## Measured text reduction

| Scope                              |       Before |        After |
| ---------------------------------- | -----------: | -----------: |
| Visible article prose and captions | 64,235 words | 60,519 words |
| Captions alone, included above     |  4,546 words |  2,484 words |

The pass removes 3,716 words, or 5.8% under the recorded counting method. Caption text falls by approximately 45%. These are text-volume measures, not measured improvements in reading time or comprehension.

The count uses the same whitespace-based method before and after: frontmatter, alternative text and standalone full-size/narrow view-link lines are excluded; visible captions and other article text are included. It covers the current 39 drafts and is separate from the earlier consolidation's 36-pattern baseline.
