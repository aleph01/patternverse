## Practice boundaries

Personal worksheets apply underlying principles; facilitator guides support real people on paper, in a room, or through an external call. Preparation, performed, and passed paths are distinct. Unperformed activities do not solicit invented observations or scores. Roles and proposed actions do not create another person’s commitment. Timers are optional local cues. Spiral Journal records only selected insights, with four optional entries; it does not collect the full private paper page. STAR keeps four individual ratings separate and does not calculate a team-quality total.

25/10 uses physical cards, five independently chosen 1–5 ratings, and actual participant-supplied totals; the guide does not run digital ballots. Wise Crowds retains central consultants and satellite observers rather than substituting Troika or AI roles. Drawing Together and Folding Spectrogram retain their external physical activities; the source’s online spectrogram variant omits the fold.
