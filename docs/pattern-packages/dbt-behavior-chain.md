# Behavior Chain and Solution Analysis

Implemented in `content/patterns/dbt-behavior-chain.md`, in the Therapy & Wellbeing verse and the DBT collection. The public schema has no recommendation-query field; these editorial retrieval examples are kept here rather than adding unsupported metadata.

## Recommendation queries

- I keep sending messages I regret when I'm overwhelmed.
- How can I understand what happened before I snapped?
- I know what I want to stop doing but can't see where to change it.
- I feel as though my reaction came out of nowhere.
- I want to understand why walking away felt helpful at first.
- How do I learn from a difficult interaction without attacking myself?
- I keep abandoning tasks after getting frustrated.
- I can spot the pattern afterward but need a plan for next time.
- What could I change before another stressful conversation?
- I want to repair a mistake and reduce the chance of repeating it.

## Visual decision

Process/flow: a series of linked nodes with an accented alternative branching from an intermediate node. The sequence represents a working chain; the branch represents a possible intervention rather than an inevitable outcome or a proven cause. Implemented in the existing concept-graphic registry, with no new rendering component.

## Practice decisions

- The article preserves the eight requested editorial sections and the established eight-part chain/solution sequence.
- The practice uses the shared Pattern Language runtime, with no AI or external service.
- Personal, private-notes, and fictional-example routes share optional prompts. The example is not silently inserted into a personal summary.
- Links are a participant-ordered, bounded collection, with explicit gap/uncertainty notes and no causal inference.
- The runner provides pause/end controls; authored early-completion branches also appear after chain review and reflection.
- Completion repeats entered material and selected responses. Repair is never presumed, and rehearsal is not treated as real-world action.
