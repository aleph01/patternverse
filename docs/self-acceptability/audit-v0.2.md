# Editorial coverage audit, version 0.2

Presentation follow-up: article selection controls were subsequently removed at the author’s request. The content register below is unchanged; all 317 examples remain available through browsing, search and expandable details.

Nine domains and all 63 subfamilies remain active. The library has 317 examples: the original 63 statements retained under their original IDs, plus 254 new statements with explicit stable suffix IDs. Every subfamily contains five or six examples. No original statement was revised or excluded; no subfamily was merged or moved.

The original typed catalogue and article are preserved in history/library-v0.1.json and history/article-v0.1.md. New IDs carry a subfamily_id link; suffixes are authored explicitly and must not be regenerated from array order.

## Changes

- Subfamily headings replace a single broad statement as the article’s expansion control. Selections apply to individual examples only. Concrete new examples appear before the original broader statement.
- Added Meaning not yet named for discomfort without an established self-label. Added Fake or inauthentic so those examples need not be classified as generic inadequacy. The original eight descriptors and classifications are unchanged.
- Examples distinguish local and broader self-judgments. Original examples retain unspecified scope metadata instead of receiving a new inferred scope.
- Family-related examples distinguish reflected shame from shame about having embarrassment. S0008-E06 has its own clarification question to preserve that distinction.
- The practice uses the full expanded catalogue and favors an exact mild/local example over a stronger neighboring statement. No new import syntax, source pinning requirement or truncated catalogue was introduced.
- Saved history resumes its stored source revision and catalogue through the existing Rust replay validation. Explicit source handoff and embed restoration still require an exact source match.

## Overlap decisions

Travel, romantic, sexual, professional and social experience share Being inexperienced. Practical knowledge and etiquette sit under Not knowing. An adult beginner’s age-sensitive concern sits under Learning slowly or visibly. Clothing cost can concern material circumstances or presentation; examples distinguish those judgments. Quietness differs from lacking stories to tell. Needing assistance differs from discounting an assisted achievement. Privacy differs from having a social life of one’s own. Identical normalized statements are rejected by the generator; related propositions are preserved where their subjects or judgments differ.

## Open coverage questions

This is an editorial candidate recognition library. Four to six examples cannot enumerate every body characteristic, family circumstance, cultural setting or ordinary practical task. Generic parent statements remain available, while the new family examples cover manners, education, finances, accent and judging one’s own embarrassment. Further additions should target distinct recognizable meanings, preserve degree and scope, and avoid treating differences or practical limitations as defects. No anticipated consequences, protective requirements or actual ethical wrongdoing were added.
