## Scope and standard

Reviewed the writing move, original examples/tables, candidate purposes and player bindings across all 48 articles, together with the canonical songbook, listening guide, score data, manifest and source register. Structural checks inspect the complete article files. Musical checks cover the seed's intervals, transposition, major/minor chord spellings, durations, rests, harmonic alternatives, lyric setting and the two song maps. No confirmed note/duration error was found in the checked original examples. This does not certify every sentence or substitute for listening to every candidate.

A useful reference must identify the exact work/recording, illuminate the article's particular decision, direct attention to an audible relationship, connect that relationship to the original worked example, and state what the comparison cannot prove. Source identity, musical analysis and browser playback are separate checks. A notes/catalog/edition page is not automatically an audio source.

The table below reviews all 48 articles. **Recommended anchor means editorial fit only.** It does not mean that its exact recording, passage, interpretation and embed have all been verified. The three candidate choices per article remain in `listening-candidates.json`; they were not bulk-inserted during this audit. Detailed analysis remains unverified for that proposal, including the technically demanding choices flagged below.
