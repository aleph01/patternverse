## Distinct teaching roles

- **[P07 — Choose the Specific Word](../../content/patterns/writing-choose-the-specific-word.md):** Names the property or distinction relevant to the reader's purpose. A bookshop's replacement request distinguishes visible defects, quantity, and requested action without inventing a cause. A farewell-party scene compares the judgments carried by near synonyms. The article distinguishes lexical accuracy from the selection of a revealing detail, false certainty, technical classification, and the assumption that rarer or shorter wording is better.
- **[P09 — Cut Redundancy](../../content/patterns/writing-cut-redundancy.md):** Removes repeated meaning that contributes no useful development. A rehearsal-space briefing separates reiterated affordability claims from a supporting comparison, recommendation, and unresolved roof cost. A booking-form trial shows how compression can broaden a claim; a house scene shows repetition carrying emotional movement and rhythm. The article covers phrase, paragraph, and whole-work duplication without treating every repeated word as an error. P08 remains the narrower treatment of verbs and modifiers; the future P20 article can develop purposeful repetition more fully.
- **[P10 — Choose Terms the Reader Can Use](../../content/patterns/writing-choose-terms-the-reader-can-use.md):** Builds shared vocabulary for an audience's task. A print studio connects a proof to the document, checks, and decision it requires; a local support agreement shows why expanding an abbreviation may not explain the concept; a read-through shows when removing a term loses a needed distinction. The article concerns audience understanding rather than P07's lexical accuracy or P04's identification of referents.

The bookshop, farewell party, rehearsal-space proposal, public-space argument, booking trial, empty-house scene, print studio, support agreement, and rehearsal instructions are original, hypothetical teaching examples. Numbers, procedures, and agreements are invented. In particular, the print and support instructions describe their stated local processes rather than prescribing universal industry procedures.
