## Deliverables and boundaries

- **P08 — [Let the Verb Do the Work](../../content/patterns/writing-let-the-verb-do-the-work.md):** Accurate verb meaning explored through a developed workshop-key exchange. Variants of sliding, nudging, shoving, and putting change the relationship and the implications of the action; meaningful modifiers remain part of the choice. Revised after reader feedback that the isolated examples felt too basic or mechanical.
- **P11 — [Show What Matters, Summarize the Rest](../../content/patterns/writing-show-what-matters-summarize-the-rest.md):** One combined treatment of dramatization and allocating narrative time. A six-week arrangement stays compressed while one consequential exchange expands; a brief aftermath and a cumulative summary demonstrate different uses of compression. No second article repeats scene-versus-summary pacing.
- **P36 — [Set Up and Pay Off](../../content/patterns/writing-set-up-and-pay-off.md):** Earlier information supports a later development. An emotional return to a signed workbench leads the explanation; a separately contextualized bicycle solution and an essay demonstrate transfer. Connected prose distinguishes established conditions from remaining uncertainty; recurring motif and whole-work ending remain neighboring subjects.

## Examples and presentation

All three introductions now explain the writing problem, the pattern's usefulness, and when to apply it before introducing a developed example. Each article supplies the roles and situation needed to understand its first example; no prior acquaintance with the recurring workshop characters is assumed. This reflects the reader's preference for an orienting introduction rather than a required example-first opening.

All teaching passages and numerical scenarios are original and invented. Nila and Ruth provide a recurring fictional workshop setting; local passages illustrate alternative drafting choices rather than forming a continuous canonical story. The report and service-desk examples are hypothetical, not evidence about actual organizations.

The examples deliberately perform different work: P08 revises wording within sentences, P11 reallocates narrative attention, and P36 establishes an earlier dependency or expectation. The articles use approximately 1,100–1,300 words each, with complete worked comparisons and explicit limits. Exercises remain separate from these explanatory articles.

The full articles have now been reworked around explanation followed by independently contextualized examples, rather than applying that approach only to the introductions. Each section explains the relevant choice before developing a passage and its tradeoffs. P08 uses connected prose comparisons; P11 and P36 no longer use tables that repeat the prose. There is no separate diagram or image asset. This is a judgment about these articles, not a collection-wide prohibition on useful tables.
