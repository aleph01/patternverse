# Musical composition: example map

Working proposal · 29 October 2026

[Player requirements derived from this map](player-requirements-from-examples.md) · [Structured records](example-map.json) · [Taxonomy and coverage review](coverage-review.md)

## Direction: recognizable music, played locally

Prefer a curated MIDI excerpt of a recognizable work when pitch, rhythm, harmony, independent parts or form is the teaching mechanism. Show the actual passage first; optionally compare a clearly labeled original transformation. The music should be identifiable as a specific work/version, with an accurate transcription and a usable source. A synthesized rendering is a study of the selected notes and timing, not a substitute for the production or performance.

For timbre, space, sampling and vocal delivery, use original or cleared local audio. External recordings remain optional context. Existing listening studies are retained as editorial leads; a source article mentioning a work does not prove that work demonstrates every candidate linked to the article.

This map covers **60 proposed core candidates and all 49 current articles**. They overlap; this is not 109 articles. **16 current articles** have an additional brief outside the core shortlist, including scale building, intervals, chord families and transposition. No article, collection assignment, playback implementation or media asset changes in this pass.

## How to read the map

Every entry names a musical question, a concrete reference/changed comparison or sequence, what remains fixed, a listening cue, a suggested span and the minimum presentation capabilities. Durations are authoring targets, not researched timestamps. All original alternatives below are briefs; they are not newly composed or rendered assets.

The repertoire column selects a work or teaching reference, not a completed MIDI transcription. Existing written studies are carried forward without a fresh audio audition. Newly checked source pages establish only the bounded facts recorded in the structured map. Where no fitting passage is established, the original demonstration carries the example while repertoire selection remains open.
