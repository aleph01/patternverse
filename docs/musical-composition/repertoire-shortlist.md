## Direction

Use recognizable music inside the local player for the musical relationship a lesson teaches. Prefer a short, reviewed excerpt over a full-song MIDI whose relevant parts are missing. Keep the original/performance reference available as context. MIDI can replace many video-led note, rhythm, harmony and form demonstrations; it cannot replace vocal delivery, sampled-source identity, spatial sound, lyrical meaning or documentary evidence of a creative process.

This shortlist covers **all 49 current lessons**, each with **one to three ranked choices**, and cross-references **all 60 proposed composition patterns**. It builds on the earlier listening research; it is not a fresh audition of every work. The current lesson order and taxonomy remain unchanged.

## Priority example: Videotape at Bonnaroo

The user confirmed **Radiohead at Bonnaroo, 17 June 2006**. Keep that exact performance identity throughout the example.

- [Official performance library route](https://www.radiohead.com/library/#ir/2006-06-17-bonnaroo) establishes the intended recording route; its page is JavaScript-driven.
- [Berklee’s contextual study](https://www.berklee.edu/berklee-now/news/vox-radiohead-s-secret-rhythm) identifies Videotape’s hidden rhythmic relationship as a teaching topic. It does not verify our future transcription.
- A [generic community Videotape sequence](https://onlinesequencer.net/4128452) advertises MIDI export. Its version/parts are unverified; direct retrieval returned 403.
- A [Bonnaroo-specific drum chart](https://supersimpledrum.com/songs/videotape-bonnaroo-version-radiohead) advertises MIDI/MusicXML. It is a useful exact-version lead, but direct retrieval returned 403 and its notes have not been inspected.
- [Faber’s official piano-solo score](https://www.fabermusic.com/shop/videotape-d4589) is an artist-approved reference. The listing does not identify it as the Bonnaroo version; no purchase was made.

The proposed local example is **piano alone → the same piano with a verified beat reference**. An optional on-beat counterexample is an explicitly authored transformation. Locate the beat from the actual live drums/bass before aligning the piano. Any added click is our guide, not a part claimed to be in the recording. Choose the exact passage before making a bar/timestamp claim.

## MIDI acquisition routes

| Route                            | Use                                                                                                            | Current evidence                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open score + matching MIDI       | Start with explicit part structure and score-backed notes.                                                     | Five exact Mutopia files are retained for research. Two editions carry CC BY-SA 3.0; the others have a Public Domain label.                                                |
| Existing song MIDI               | Inspect the actual file for required parts, timing, version and provenance.                                    | Superstition provider demo parsed; 45 seconds and seven note-bearing tracks.                                                                                               |
| Community analysis / export lead | A cross-check or a route to a small curated excerpt, never unquestioned ground truth.                          | TheoryTab pages retrieved for Videotape, Superstition, Lean on Me, Avril 14th, Seven Nation Army, Space Oddity and Get Lucky. No exported MIDI retrieved from those pages. |
| Publisher score                  | A reliable candidate source for a reviewed transcription; confirm the particular arrangement and usable terms. | Faber lists an artist-approved Videotape piano score; exact Bonnaroo equivalence is not established.                                                                       |
| New short transcription          | Prefer this when available files flatten the important rhythm, omit the melody, or use another version.        | Prioritize the exact musical relationship and required reference parts rather than recreating every production detail.                                                     |

Record the composition/transcription source, arrangement/version, credit, terms, selected passage and changes once per asset. A download/purchase, a parsed file and permission to embed it in this product are separate statuses. This is an acquisition record, not an approval gate for ordinary research.
