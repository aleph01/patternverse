# Legal release review

The public notices describe the implementation as inspected on 2 October 2026.
They are not a legal opinion, a compliance certificate, or a liability waiver.

Before relying on this release as a complete German/EU compliance setup:

- Confirm a functioning, regularly checked contact address. `contact@patternverse.org`
  was bouncing with `550 5.7.1 Relaying denied` when these notices were drafted.
- Review the Impressum for additional obligations applicable to the operator
  (for example VAT/business identification or journalistic-editorial responsibility).
  No registration, qualifications, or identifiers have been invented.
- Verify the applicable Cloudflare contracting entity, processing agreement,
  enabled dashboard products (including analytics/cookies), actual log retention,
  subprocessors, and international-transfer safeguards. Source inspection alone
  cannot verify account-level configuration or an executed processing agreement.
- Review controller/processor roles and contractual arrangements for OpenRouter,
  individual model providers, optional Dropbox sync, and email forwarding/Gmail.
  No promise of EU-only processing or universal zero retention is made.
- Obtain specialist advice on sensitive personal/health data (GDPR Article 9),
  lawful bases, an appropriate consent flow if needed, and international transfers.
  A warning not to enter medical data is not a substitute for a lawful basis.
  This release adds notices, not a new consent-management system or DPIA.
- Review actual Mira functions, prompts, therapy-named approaches, library content,
  and marketing for medical-device/healthcare and AI transparency requirements.
  A disclaimer alone does not determine the legal classification of software.
- Verify the operator's email-retention practice matches the published criterion.
  Keep the notices current whenever processing or providers change.
