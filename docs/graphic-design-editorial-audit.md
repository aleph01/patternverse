# Graphic Design editorial consolidation audit

**Implementation update:** the approved recommendations have now been applied locally. The analysis below records the pre-consolidation review; current status is in [the consolidation record](pattern-packages/graphic-design-consolidation.json).

Reviewed 28 October 2026. **58 candidates become 39 proposed core patterns: 36 based on existing articles and 3 provisional planned cores.** The canonical articles, routes and visual assets have not been changed by this audit.

## The editorial test

For every candidate, identify its distinct decision, recognizable failure and repair. Then ask whether that intervention earns a separate article or mainly implements, diagnoses or introduces another decision. A narrow intervention can still stand alone; related subject matter is not sufficient reason to merge.

The review covers the openings/core interventions, worked-example structures, relevant repair passages and package metadata for all 52 drafts. Additional passages were read to resolve overlap. This is a structural editorial audit, not a full copy edit, source revalidation or new visual inspection. The six unwritten candidates have title-level, provisional verdicts.

The target count was not used as a quota. Distinctions that survived the test remain even where a more aggressive merge would create a smaller index.

## The three combined articles

| Proposed core | Source material | Editorial contract |
|---|---|---|
| **Establish Visual Hierarchy** | Establish a Focal Point + Differentiate Levels of Importance; scale becomes a technique. | Choose what leads, define supporting ranks, and make those roles dependable. Use scale as one lever rather than teaching hierarchy again. |
| **Use Spacing to Express Relationships** | Use Negative Space + Establish Consistent Spacing + Group Related Elements Through Proximity. | Allocate pauses, make within-group distances tighter than between-group distances, and repeat equivalent intervals. Explain that consistent spacing does not mean equal spacing everywhere. |
| **Build a Purposeful Palette** | Limit the Palette + Use Complementary Contrast + Build Analogous Harmony. | Choose a working set that provides the required unity and distinction. Related and opposing hues are options with tradeoffs; tonal legibility belongs to Value Contrast. |

## Two articles whose examples need different homes

- **Frame the Content:** examples 1 and 3 concern membership and redundant boxes; place them with Group Elements Within Shared Boundaries. Example 2 protects text over a photograph; place it with Separate Figure from Ground and its background-treatment technique. A generic framing article would continue mixing these jobs.
- **Guide the Eye:** place gaze and useful directional flow under Establish a Clear Reading Order. Place exact-feature leader/annotation material under Integrate Text and Image. Keep one primary home for each comparison rather than duplicating all three in both destinations.

## Distinctions that survive the test

- **Alignment and grids:** a drifting left edge or numeric right edge can be repaired locally; a grid defines a reusable allocation framework. Alignment stays independent, revising the earlier tentative merge suggestion.
- **Sequence and reading order:** one chooses the logic of the message; the other makes an already chosen sequence traversable.
- **Continuity and reading flow:** connector crossings and partly hidden contours require a specific path interpretation, not a predicted gaze route.
- **Line length, leading, letter spacing and line breaks:** each has an independently observable failure and a repair at a different level. Keep them independently findable, but make their treatments shorter.
- **Type scale and hierarchy:** a reusable role vocabulary across formats differs from choosing one composition’s priority; the scale article stays.
- **Implied forms and abstraction:** partly hiding an existing form while supporting its continuation differs from replacing incidental appearance with a selected model.
- **Value contrast and meaning beyond color:** separation between values does not provide an independent way to identify hue-coded meaning. Both stay.
- **Symbols and metaphors:** using a learned association differs from proposing a cross-domain comparison.
