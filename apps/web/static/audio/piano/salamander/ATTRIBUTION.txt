The 30 MP3 files are copied unchanged from that distribution. This compact bank
uses one recording per sampled pitch, not all velocity layers of the full library.
During playback, nearby pitches are obtained by resampling; gain and a short
note-release envelope are applied. No endorsement by the author is implied.
