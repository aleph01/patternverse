Explore ways of relating to thoughts and feelings while moving toward what matters. These original scripted adaptations cover the six broad ACT processes, not a diagnosis-specific protocol. There is no requirement to feel better, agree with a thought, or tolerate harmful circumstances.

See [ACBS's introduction to ACT](https://contextualscience.org/about_act) and Hayes, Strosahl & Wilson (2012), *Acceptance and Commitment Therapy*, second edition, for the underlying approach. The exercises here are not individually clinically validated.
